# 1.2.01
Released 2026-11-04

## Barbeque's Delight <!-- icon: barbequesdelight:grill -->

- **Changed:** WTHIT shows "Burnt" for a burnt skewer on a Grill ["Burnt!!"]
- **Fixed:** Food seasoned with a Barbeque's Delight powder or sauce, and grilled skewers, showed no seasoning sparkle; they show the salt sparkle
- **Fixed:** Skewering accepted only one item of each ingredient; any item of the ingredient works (both beef, both cabbage, minced beef, cod slices and so on)
- **New:** Raw Potato Skewers take Sweet Potatoes as well as Potatoes
- **Fixed:** The Kebab Sandwich and Bibimbap accepted only one kind of cooked skewer; any cooked skewer works
- **New:** Sneak + right click on an Ingredients Basin takes one item into the inventory with items in hand
- **New:** Spruce, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Crimson and Warped Ingredients Basins and Trays: work like the Ingredients Basin and Tray; stonecut from that wood's logs
- **Changed:** The Ingredients Basin and Tray are named Oak Ingredients Basin and Oak Tray, and are stonecut from oak logs or any log without a basin and tray of its own [any log]
- **Changed:** Trays have a texture of their own in each wood [Farmer's Delight crate bottom], and stack on a tray of any wood
- **Changed:** The JEI Skewering page shows an off-hand icon beside the off-hand slot, and the JEI Grilling page shows the Grilled seasoning on the result

## Interface & Tooltips <!-- icon: minecraft:written_book -->

- **Fixed:** The Seasoning, Canning, Using Cans and Feast Plating JEI pages had no title, and other text from the pack's own mods was missing
- **Fixed:** On a server, JEI's villager trade pages showed only vanilla trades; they show the pack's trades, Barbeque's Delight seasonings and salt included

## Corn <!-- icon: creativeengineering:corncob -->

- **Changed:** A Corncob on the Cutting Board gives 2 Corn Kernels [6]
- **New:** A Corncob gives 3 Corn Kernels in the Mortar and Pestle, and 4 Corn Kernels with a 50% chance of Cornmeal from the Crushing Wheels, Millstone and Crusher

## Curios <!-- icon: simplehats:smokingpipe -->

- **Fixed:** The Face slot showed a missing texture; it shows the helmet outline
- **New:** The Simple Hats Smoking Pipe fits the Face slot

## Jams <!-- icon: vintagedelight:cherry_jam_jar -->

- **Fixed:** Jam Blocks and jam fluids of the ten new jams showed a broken stripe texture in the world and in JEI; they use Fruits Delight's jam block pattern
- **Changed:** Jello Blocks and melted jello of those jams use Fruits Delight's jello block pattern [the jam swirl]
- **Changed:** Jam jars, Jam Blocks, Jello Blocks, bowls of Jello and fruit cauldrons of those jams are recoloured to match their fruit
- **Changed:** The Empty Mason Jar uses Fruits Delight's empty jar sprite
- **Changed:** The Honey, Apple Sauce, Nut Mash, Nut Milk, Relish and Vinegar Jars use the new mason jar art
- **Changed:** Placed jars of Fruits Delight jams show the new jam art inside

## Cutting Boards <!-- icon: farmersdelight:oak_cutting_board -->

- **New:** Oak, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Crimson and Warped Cutting Boards: work like the Cutting Board; crafted from 2 sticks and 4 of that wood's planks
- **Changed:** The Cutting Board is named Spruce Cutting Board and takes spruce planks or any planks without a board of their own [any planks]
- **Fixed:** Granite, Andesite and Diorite Cutting Boards on a counter sat at the plate position; they sit where the Cutting Board does
