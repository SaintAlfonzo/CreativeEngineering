# 1.0.4
Released 2026-10-04

All changes since 1.0.3.

## Wandering Trader

- **Changed:** The Wandering Trader and the Wandering Winemaker spawn on separate timers, each about once per 19 hours of play [one shared roll, about 38 hours each].
- **Changed:** Wandering Traders carry 25 common and 5 rare offers [5 and 1].
- **Changed:** Wandering Trader stock: saplings, tall flowers, seeds, plants, natural blocks, mob buckets and a random golden food added; computer, currency machine and Nether items removed.
- **New:** The Wandering Winemaker sells Fruits Delight tree saplings and white jungle grape seeds.

## Hoes and Farming <!-- icon: minecraft:oak_leaves -->

- **New:** Each hoe harvests and replants a square: 1x1 wood and stone, 2x2 copper and gold, 3x3 iron, steel and manasteel, 4x4 diamond, netherite and elementium, 5x5 echo, felsic and dragon. Shown in the tooltip.
- **Changed:** Tobacco grows on Rich Farmland and Fertile Soil, keeps farmland from drying out, and works with right-click replanting and hoe harvesting.

## Wild Crops <!-- icon: minecraft:grass_block -->

- **Changed:** Every wild crop and wild fruit tree grows in biomes chosen by climate and ground. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Rubber trees grow only in jungles. New chunks only.
- **Removed:** Wild coffee in the Nether. New chunks only.
- **Removed:** Apples from oak and dark oak leaves, and bananas from leaves.

## Structures <!-- icon: minecraft:amethyst_cluster -->

- **New:** 1 pillager outpost in 8 is an Improved Pillager Outpost tower. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Oceans and rivers only get water, sky and underground structures; pillager towers no longer spawn in the sea. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Structures whose foundation would hang over water or air are not placed. Ships, wrecks and sky structures are exempt. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Bank vaults are about 5 times rarer in villages. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Driftwood: floating driftwood at a quarter of the rate, and driftwood on land at about a tenth of that. New chunks only.

## Camera Bags <!-- icon: minecraft:paper -->

- **New:** Camera bags take dyes in the crafting grid and wash in a cauldron. A smithing upgrade keeps the colour and the contents.
- **Changed:** All three camera bags use the leather bag look [iron and netherite used lunchbox textures].

## Wine <!-- icon: brewinandchewin:keg -->

- **Changed:** The Aging Barrel keeps making wine while the output slot holds the same wine of the same year [stopped after one].
- **Changed:** Wine effects gain a level every 2 years of age [4].

## Loot <!-- icon: minecraft:chest -->

- **Removed:** The Golden Food enchantment book from all loot.
- **Changed:** Chests and barrels in structures that held fixed items are per-player loot chests.

## Gear & Combat <!-- icon: minecraft:shield -->

- **Fixed:** Dragon horse armor gave the horse no effect. It gives Regeneration.

## Mobs & Animals

- **New:** Right-click a canned golem with a dye to recolour it.

## Interface & Tooltips

- **Fixed:** Placed Display Delight foods showed a purple and black cube, or no icon, in WTHIT and JEI.

## Performance & Stability <!-- icon: minecraft:clock -->

- **Fixed:** Players were kicked when joining the server.
- **Fixed:** The game crashed at startup.

## Mods Added & Removed

- **New:** Blue Flame Burning, Fruits Delight, Improved Pillager Outpost, Just a Well.
- **Removed:** Dimensional Gravity.
