# 1.0.2
Released 2026-09-30

All changes since 1.0.1. Clients only: the server got these with 1.0.3.

## Cans <!-- icon: minecraft:iron_ingot -->

- **New:** Cans hold several servings. Eating one takes a serving, a bar shows what is left, and opened cans are eaten first.
- **New:** The last serving of a can leaves an Empty Can (70%), a Crushed Empty Can (25%) or nothing.
- **New:** 36 canned foods made from raw vegetables, fruit or meat. Recipes with Salt, Sugar or Bone Broth take less of the food.
- **Removed:** Canned Goods' food can recipes, and smelting cans back into iron.
- **Changed:** Empty Cans: 4 per Iron Ingot in the Stonecutter, 12 from 2 iron plates in the crafting grid or with a Deployer, 32 from 4 iron plates in the Engineer's Workbench.

## Flowers <!-- icon: minecraft:poppy -->

- **Changed:** Bushier Flowers' bushes no longer grow from bone meal (Flower Patch growth instead) or generate in the world. New chunks only.
- **New:** 4 of a flower craft its Bushier Flowers bush (4 Poppy make Poppies).

## Food & Cooking <!-- icon: farmersdelight:cooking_pot -->

- **Fixed:** Fried Eggs could not be made in a furnace, smoker or on a campfire.
- **Changed:** Cooking Oil also takes Pumpkin Seeds. Heated Mixer: 3 seeds + 1 bottle give 1 [6 seeds + 1 bottle give 2].

## Mobs & Animals <!-- icon: minecraft:squid_spawn_egg -->

- **New:** Emu Nest: bark in the Hay Nest's U shape. Emus lay their eggs in it.

## Gameplay

- **Changed:** More items fill the Composter: all flowers and produce crates, sacks and baskets at 100%, Tobacco Seeds, and many modded foods and plants.
- **Fixed:** Most of the pack's chest loot changes did not apply on servers.
- **Fixed:** The Mechanical Engineer villager had no trades.

## Performance & Stability <!-- icon: minecraft:clock -->

- **Fixed:** The server crashed with a random number error from Regrowth.
- **Fixed:** The server froze when hostile mobs spawned near campfires at the edge of loaded chunks.

## Mods Added & Removed

- **New:** Untitled Duck Mod, Fuel Goes Here, Async Locator.
- **Removed:** No Hostiles Around Campfire (caused the campfire freeze), Snow Under Trees.
