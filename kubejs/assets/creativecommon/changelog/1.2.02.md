- **New:** Alex's Mobs Crows drop Crow Feathers (Feathers).
- **New:** 5 juices on the Apple Press, 6 wines in the Fermentation Barrel, Root Beer and Moonshine in the Keg.

- **New:** Fluid Jug: holds 8 buckets of one fluid, placeable, keeps its fluid when broken or picked up.

- **New:** Bottle Crate: slab-shaped, 9 bottles or eggs per half, moved by hoppers.
- **New:** Salt Blocks wear away when licked by you or nearby animals, up to three stages, and pop 1 salt at the end. Honeycomb waxes a block, an axe removes the wax, and salt restores one stage.
- **New:** Salt Block from 4 Raw Rock Salt.
- **New:** Scarecrow, crafted with any farmer's hat. Scarecrows, carved pumpkins and jack o'lanterns keep crows away.
- **New:** Wild crows flee players and villagers, approach food, peck crops back to their first stage, and steal 1 shiny item from the hotbar, dropped when hit or after 60 seconds.
- **New:** Hand-fed, thrown or shiny items can tame a crow; tame crows breed on corn.
- **New:** Nest, holds 4 items and generates on the ground; crows may stash loot in it.
- **New:** Horseshoe fits a tamed horse: +25 % speed, Fire Resistance, no cactus or berry damage, no slowdown on soul sand or honey. Thrown at a fence it lights touching lamps.
- **New:** Sugar Horse Treat boosts a horse's speed and jump by 25 % for 3 minutes, stacking with the Horseshoe and Sugar Cube.
- **New:** Fertilizer Bag, 8 uses: matures crops and grows saplings and stems.
- **New:** Farmer's Hat; it and the straw and farmer hats grant XP when harvesting ripe crops.
- **New:** Tilling enchantment I to III on hoes: sneak-use tills 3x3, 4x4 or 5x5. Tilling end stone makes Tilled End Stone.
- **New:** Hops grow on grapevine lattices over farmland. Horses eat them to heal.
- **New:** Sunflower Seeds plant a stem that grows into a sunflower. Blueberry Pips plant blueberry bushes.
- **New:** Hold a cooked marshmallow near a fire for 10 seconds to char it.
- **New:** Potions of Pungent, Tempting and Bad Omen. Bad Omen brewed with a Fermented Spider Eye gives Luck.
- **New:** Brine in a keg or mixer, juices pressed by compacting, Sap to Syrup in a heated mixer.
- **New:** Manure works in the Garden Cloche and Organic Compost.
- **New:** Wild Hops in forests, Mums in plains, forests and meadows, Salt Caves under dry, desert, savanna and beach biomes, and the Corn Maze in plains.
- **New:** Four-Leaf Clover can be placed and generates rarely in flower forests and meadows.
- **New:** Farmers buy Hops, Raspberries and Sunflower Seeds; Botanists sell Mums; Wandering Traders sell Hops and pips.
- **Changed:** Seed Pouch tops up from matching seeds picked up on the hotbar, plants from a dispenser, and plants 5x5 [3x3].
- **Changed:** Macaroni and Cheese, Glazed Carrots and Biscuits and Gravy saturation 0.75 [0.5].
- **Changed:** Scared Still is no longer applied by the tiger or the dragon shovel.
- **New:** 2 % chance of a Cherry from Cherry Leaves, not with shears.
