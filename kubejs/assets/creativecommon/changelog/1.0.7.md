# 1.0.7
Released 2026-10-18

All changes since 1.0.6.

## Cooking Stations <!-- icon: farmersdelight:cooking_pot -->

- **New:** Oven: bakes like a Cooking Pot. Needs fire, a campfire, lava, a Magma Block or a Blaze Burner below, and heats a pot placed on top like a Stove.
- **Changed:** The Cooking Pot, the Kettle and the Oven keep leftover bottles, bowls and buckets in two leftover slots instead of throwing them out. A full leftover slot pauses cooking.

## World Generation <!-- icon: minecraft:amethyst_cluster -->

- **Fixed:** The same seed built different villages after each server restart. New chunks only.
- **Fixed:** Cloud Storage and Domestication Innovation village houses were lost when the world reloaded.
- **Fixed:** Beach, river and swamp driftwood never generated. It lies on banks next to water. New chunks only.
- **Fixed:** Skeleton Dungeon, Bone Dungeon and Lost Soul City never generated. New chunks only.
- **Fixed:** The Ruined Drum Tower could hang over cliffs. New chunks only.

## Gameplay

- **Fixed:** Quark's sort button deleted items from containers holding stacks larger than the normal maximum.
- **Fixed:** The Exposure camera viewfinder stayed up after respawning, changing dimension or rejoining, and took clicks inside open screens.

## Performance & Stability <!-- icon: minecraft:clock -->

- **Fixed:** Structure searches (explorer maps, map trades, `/locate`) stalled the server while unexplored chunks generated.

## Mods Added & Removed

- **New:** Create: The Salt and Storage Labels.
- **Changed:** Let's Do Vinery 1.3.10 replaced by a backport of Vinery 1.5.5, with its new blocks and wines. Let's Do API removed.
- **Changed:** Brewin' and Chewin' replaced by a backport of its 4.5.0 version (was 2.0).
- **New:** Fancy Crops and Fancy Beds resource packs, off by default.
