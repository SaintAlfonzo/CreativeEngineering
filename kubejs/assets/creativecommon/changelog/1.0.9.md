# 1.0.9
Released 2026-10-19

All changes since 1.0.8.

## Nether Blocks in the Overworld <!-- icon: minecraft:netherrack -->

- **Changed:** Scarlet Mountains: Jasper with 10% Crimsite [Netherrack]. No Crimson Nylium or crimson plant patches. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Moonlight Grove and Moonlight Valley: grass [Warped Nylium], no warped roots or nether sprouts. The blue flower patches grow on the grass. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Mirage Isles: enchanted oak trees have Stripped Acacia Log trunks. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Create: Dreams & Desires' layered rock in the Overworld is Granite only [breccia, gabbro, granite and glowstone layers]. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Structures: Soul Fire is Fire and Glowstone is Yellow Corundum. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Red roof castles: Magma Block [Netherrack]. New chunks only.
- **Removed:** Nether Wart from underground bunkers. New chunks only.

## Biomes <!-- icon: minecraft:grass_block -->

- **Changed:** Each rare Overworld surface biome covers at least 0.15% of the world, each cave biome at least 1.2%. The 12 largest land biomes are about a quarter smaller. New worlds only.
- **Fixed:** 9 surface biomes never generated. New worlds only.

## Crafting & Recipes <!-- icon: minecraft:crafting_table -->

- **Fixed:** The Oven's [+] button in JEI did nothing.
- **Fixed:** Planks had no Immersive Engineering Crusher recipe. They crush into sawdust like logs.
- **New:** 9 Copper Plates craft a copper plate block.
- **Removed:** Crushing zinc ore with the Engineer's Hammer, as for the other metals.

## World Generation <!-- icon: minecraft:amethyst_cluster -->

- **Fixed:** Campsite Structures camps stood on a 4-block dirt plinth above the ground around them. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Structures that fill in the ground under themselves, such as camps and ruins, are not placed where water covers over 60% of their footprint. New chunks only.

## Gear & Combat <!-- icon: minecraft:shield -->

- **Fixed:** Stockpile, Recovery and Cycle enchantments still rolled on randomly enchanted loot.
- **Fixed:** Splash and lingering Potion Bucket tooltips showed the base effect duration, not the applied one (x1.1 splash, x1.2 lingering).

## Performance & Stability <!-- icon: minecraft:clock -->

- **Fixed:** Teleporting outside the End (`/tp`, Ender Pearls, Chorus Fruit) into unexplored terrain froze the server, sometimes long enough to crash it.
- **Fixed:** The server crashed when an item fell into fire or lava.

## Mods Added & Removed

- **Removed:** Rain Should Extinguish Campfires. Unlit Campfire already puts campfires out in rain.
