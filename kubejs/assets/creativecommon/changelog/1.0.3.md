# 1.0.3
Released 2026-10-02

All changes since 1.0.2.

## Cameras and Photography <!-- icon: minecraft:paper -->

- **Changed:** Exposure updated to 1.9.19 [1.6.0]. Cameras and photos from the old version do not carry over.
- **New:** Exposure Polaroid: Instant Camera and instant slides.
- **New:** Exposure: Expanded: 2 lenses, 16 filters, new film types and 6 instant slides.
- **New:** Leather, Iron and Netherite Camera Bags: 5, 9 and 14 slots for cameras, the camera stand, lenses, filters, film and instant slides. Leather is crafted; Iron and Netherite are smithing upgrades that keep the contents.

## Trash Slot <!-- icon: minecraft:chest -->

- **Changed:** The trash slot is on by default in the player and creative inventories, chests, barrels, ender chests, vanilla workstations, the brewing stand and Sophisticated Backpacks [player inventory, crafting table and chests only].
- **Changed:** Each Sophisticated Backpacks tier has its own trash slot position, starting under the backpack's bottom-right corner.
- **New:** The creative inventory has a trash slot on every tab, and the survival inventory shows it for creative players.
- **Fixed:** The trash slot could sit off screen at high GUI scales.

## Food & Cooking <!-- icon: farmersdelight:cooking_pot -->

- **Changed:** Vegetable and meat can recipes take Salt or Bone Broth; fruit can recipes take Sugar or a Honey Bottle, which is returned. Canned Pumpkin moved from salt to sugar.
- **New:** Canned Cookies: Empty Can + 8 Cookies, or 6 Cookies + Sugar.
- **New:** Canned Bread: Empty Can + 6 Bread.
- **New:** Culture Blocks for the 7 cultured meats: 9 cultures make 1 block, and 1 block gives back 9.
- **Changed:** Canned Rotten Flesh renamed Canned Tough Jerky, Canned Spider Eyes renamed Canned Night Berries, Chocolate Spider Eye renamed Chocolate Night Berries.

## World Generation <!-- icon: minecraft:grass_block -->

- **Changed:** Overworld biome sizes re-tuned. New worlds only.

## Gameplay <!-- icon: minecraft:red_bed -->

- **Changed:** Join protection lasts up to 150 seconds [60]. It still ends as soon as you move.

## Interface & Tooltips <!-- icon: minecraft:written_book -->

- **New:** JEI pages for Vinery's Aging Barrel, Apple Press, Grape Stomping and Wine Aging.
- **Fixed:** Display Delight placed foods and Rustic Delight's coffee, cotton and bell pepper crops and potted wild plants showed raw names.

## Performance & Stability <!-- icon: minecraft:clock -->

- **Fixed:** Lightman's Currency cashier and Cloud Storage balloon salesman map trades searched for structures on the server thread, freezing the server.

## Mods Added & Removed

- **New:** Farmer's Respite, Corn Delight, More Delight, Mysterious Mountain Lib.
