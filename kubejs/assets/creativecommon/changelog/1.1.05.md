# 1.1.05
Released 2026-10-31

## Lunchboxes <!-- icon: solapplepie:netherite_lunchbox -->

- **New:** Netherite Lunchbox: 18 slots in two rows of 9, fire resistant. Smithing: Iron Lunchbox + Netherite Ingot, with a Netherite Upgrade template.
- **Changed:** Lunchbox renamed Copper Lunchbox, with a copper texture. Recipe: Bundle + 6 Copper Ingots (was Lunchbag + 6 Iron Ingots).
- **Changed:** Golden Lunchbox renamed Iron Lunchbox, with an iron texture. Recipe: Copper Lunchbox + 6 Iron Ingots (was Lunchbox + 6 Gold Ingots).
- **New:** Lunchboxes take dyes in the crafting grid, mixed like leather armor. Only the base changes color; outline and latches stay.
- **New:** Lunchbox skins: smithing an empty lower-tier lunchbox or a Fantasy Furniture berry basket onto a lunchbox gives it that look. The skin item is used up.
- **New:** Lunchbox + Soap in the crafting grid: removes the dye, or the skin when it has no dye.
- **New:** Lunchbox tooltip: number of slots.
- **Fixed:** Upgrading a lunchbox deleted the food inside.
- **Fixed:** Seagulls stole lunchboxes from player inventories. Seagulls now ignore lunchboxes, carried or dropped.

## Powdered Sugar

- **Changed:** Powdered Sugar from the crusher and Millstone: 1 Sugar gives 1 Powdered Sugar and a 50% chance of a second (was 1).
- **Changed:** Mortar and pestle: 3 Sugar give 4 Powdered Sugar (was 1 Sugar gives 1).
- **Changed:** Powdered Sugar Bucket: a bucket and 4 Sugar in the Vacuum Chamber (was 3 Sugar). Emptied in the crafting grid it gives 8 Powdered Sugar (was 3).
- **New:** Bucket + 8 Powdered Sugar in the crafting grid gives a Powdered Sugar Bucket.
- **Removed:** Fishing up a Powdered Sugar Bucket.
- **Changed:** Powdered Sugar block texture (was recolored powder snow).

## Salads

- **Changed:** Coleslaw: 12 hunger, 14.4 saturation (was 11, 13.2).
- **Changed:** Field Salad takes any berry; carrot, onion, beetroot, corn or turnip; acorn or peanut; cactus steak, broccoli, cauliflower, zucchini, cucumber or green bell pepper. Zucchini slices and cauliflower florets also work.
- **New:** Field Salad recipe with 2 flowers in place of the salad greens.
- **Changed:** Seagrass Salad takes 2 Seagrass [1].

## Jams

- **Removed:** Vintage Delight jam bottles and the Relish Bottle; those jams come in mason jars only.
- **Removed:** Sweet Berries Jam Jar and Glow Berries Jam Jar.
- **Changed:** Relish Jar no longer counts as a jam and cannot be fermented into vinegar.
- **New:** Green Grape, Red Grape and Cherry Jam Jars: Cooking Pot, 4 grapes or cherries + sugar + lemon slice gives 2 jars. Placeable like the other jam jars.
- **Changed:** PB and J takes any jam (was sweet jam bottles).

## Stepstools

- **New:** Stepstools, one per colour, crafted from a Create seat on top of an oak fence. Worn in the new Curios Feet slot.
- **New:** Stand still and hold Sprint + Jump with a Stepstool worn to stand on it, a block higher. Moving, crouching, jumping or taking damage steps off. The key can be changed in Controls ("Step Up").

## Smoked Wood

- **New:** Every Smoked block (log, wood, stripped, planks, stairs, slab, fence, fence gate, door, trapdoor, button, pressure plate) is made by smoking a block of the same shape from any wood type: smoker, campfire or Create fan with a Smoking Sail.
- **New:** Immersive Engineering Bottling Machine makes Treated Wood from planks and 125 mB creosote, same as the Create spout recipe. Smoked Planks need only 100 mB in both.

## Monster Boxes <!-- icon: quark:monster_box -->

- **Changed:** Quark Monster Boxes stay after spawning their mobs (were destroyed). Once every mob is dead or has wandered off, the box pops out one stack from its own loot table, then rests for 1 hour before it can trigger again.
- **Changed:** Mobs from a Monster Box drop only their normal loot (each also dropped a roll of the box loot).
- **Changed:** Monster Boxes mine like Obsidian and need a Diamond Pickaxe (hardness 25, any tool). Explosions no longer break them.
- **Changed:** A Monster Box only breaks while sneaking, and drops nothing; mining it standing shows a warning and leaves it in place.
- **Changed:** Create drills and saws can't break Monster Boxes, and contraptions can't move them.

## Painted Asphalt

- **Changed:** Dreams n' Desires coloured asphalt renamed Painted Asphalt. Recipe: 8 Asphalt or Painted Asphalt + 1 dye gives 8 of that colour (was slime balls, scorchia and dye).
- **New:** 8 Painted Asphalt + 1 Soap in the crafting grid gives 8 Asphalt.

## Buckets <!-- icon: creativeengineering:wooden_bucket -->

- **Changed:** Bucket recipe: 3 Iron Plates in a V shape (was 3 Iron Ingots).
- **New:** Wooden Bucket: 3 planks in a V shape. Picks up and places water only. Stacks to 16 empty.

## Crop Seasons

- **Changed:** Every crop has a growing type: temperate, cold, warm dry, warm wet, Nether, End, cave or mushroom. Each season is fertile (25% faster growth), neutral or offseason (25% slower) for that type (was: fertile seasons at normal speed, other seasons at 1/6 speed).
- **Changed:** Jungles, swamps, savannas, deserts and other tropical biomes use wet and dry seasons for crops (was: summer crops all year).
- **Changed:** Nether crops (nether wart, ghost pepper, fungus colonies, twisting and weeping vines) are fertile in the Nether and offseason elsewhere. End crops (chorus, Ender crop, chorus flame) are fertile in the End and offseason elsewhere.
- **Changed:** Glow berries and cave carrots go by height: fertile below y 0, offseason above y 128.
- **Changed:** Mushroom colonies are fertile in mushroom biomes all year.
- **Changed:** Greenhouse glass anywhere in the 16 blocks straight above a crop removes season fertility: no fertile bonus, no offseason penalty (was: always fertile).
- **Changed:** Greenhouse glass includes glass panes, glass doors, glass trapdoors and modded glass: quartz glass, rainbow glass, reinforced glass, panzer glass, frosty glass, insulating glass, slag glass, pressure chamber glass.
- **Changed:** Hemp, tobacco, Vinery apple and cherry trees and Nether's Delight fungus colonies follow the seasons (were: same speed all year).
- **Changed:** Vinery grapes go by biome, not season: fertile in their own biome type (temperate, cold, savanna or jungle), offseason in the opposite temperature (cold against savanna and jungle), neutral elsewhere (were: same speed everywhere).
- **Changed:** Seed, sapling and tree fruit tooltips show the fertile and offseason seasons (wet and dry included), biomes for grapes, or "Season: Nether", "Season: End". WTHIT shows the same on the crop, plus whether it is fertile or offseason where it stands.
- **Removed:** Fruits Delight apple trees (no longer generate; their sapling and leaves turn into the Vinery apple tree's).
- **Fixed:** The second to fourth melon, pumpkin and hamimelon from one stem grew in any season.

- **Changed:** Ham and Cheese Sandwich takes any bread (was bread or bread slice).
- **Removed:** Muffins from the Cooking Pot; they bake in the oven only.
- **Changed:** Sweet Potato Cupcake renamed Sweet Potato Muffin.
- **Changed:** Roll of Elder Guardian renamed Elder Guardian Roll.
- **Removed:** All tentacles (Miner's, Ocean's, Oceanic Delight) and the items made from them. Dishes that used tentacles take calamari.
- **New:** Truffle Pie: a placeable whole pie, baked in the oven from a pie crust, 2 truffles and 2 sugar, and eaten by the slice.

- **Fixed:** Ender's Delight additions (Amberveils, Ethereal Saffron, Chorusflame, Void Pepper and the new dishes) and Veggies Delight crop blocks showed their raw translation keys instead of names.

- **New:** Magic Resistance stops Poison and Wither damage at or below its level (Poison I with Magic Resistance I deals none). Higher levels take the 20 % per level cut.
- **Changed:** Dragon Horse Armor gives Regeneration II, Health Boost II and Resistance II (was Regeneration I).
- **Changed:** Intoxication, Travel Fatigue and Thief show their icon on the screen (were inventory only).
- **Changed:** Builder's Tea (bottle or open-ended pipe), Canned Pufferfish, Farmer's Respite and Immersive Weathering foods show their effect icons on the screen (were inventory only).
- **Changed:** Mead, Pale Jane, Saccharine Rum, Cocoa Fudge, Baklava and the Brewin' and Chewin' dishes (omelet, onion soup, pasta, lasagna, pierogi, fondue) show their effect icons on the screen (were inventory only).
- **Changed:** Agility is replaced by Climbing, same durations: bananas, banana bread, banana cake, banana ice cream and milkshake, adzuki curry.
- **Changed:** Chorus from Fruits Delight jams, jellies and jello is replaced by Teleport.
- **Removed:** Resilience. Its potions no longer brew; Quark Delight's Cooked Whole Crab gives Knockback Resistance instead.

- **Changed:** Jellie Wine gives Regeneration, Absorption, Resistance and Fire Resistance at the wine's level (was Better than Golden Apples!, which is removed).
- **Fixed:** Double Jump with Jump Boost jumped only as high as Double Jump. The higher of the two now counts.
- **New:** Compound effects list their parts under "When Applied:" in item tooltips and on their JEI page: Caffeinated, Stimulation, Recovering, Double Jump, Flashed, Sunbird's Blessing.
- **New:** Fully oxidized Everything Copper gear worn or in the hotbar or offhand can poison its holder. About every 5 minutes, each oxidized piece has a 15 % chance; Poison I for 4 s plus 2 s per extra piece, up to 20 s.

## Gameplay

- **New:** Millstone and Crushing Wheels turn any seed into Bone Meal at a 15 % chance. Corn kernels and rice are left out.
- **Changed:** Tobacco, hamimelon and lemon seeds fit in seed pouches.
- **New:** Naturally spawned cows, mooshrooms, pigs, sheep, chickens and goats have a 1 in 100 chance of a random name. Every player sees the death message when one dies.
- **Changed:** Villagers no longer walk through fence gates, open or closed.
- **Fixed:** Villagers did not use Dramatic Doors' tall and short wooden doors. They now walk through them, opening and closing them.
- **New:** Villagers climb up and down ladders to reach their beds, jobs and other targets. Vines, ropes, chains and scaffolding are not used.
- **Removed:** Neapolitan chimpanzees no longer spawn. Their spawn egg and head are hidden.
- **Fixed:** Gorillas and sugar gliders foraging leaves found the removed Alex's Mobs banana. They now find Neapolitan bananas.
- **Changed:** The armor cap is 50 points [30]. A full dragon set counts all 36 of its armor.
- **Removed:** The Sputter (arrow-firing golem built from a carved pumpkin on a barrel) can no longer be built. Existing ones stay.
- **New:** Operators can move the calendar with `/cetime date`: back to the world's start, to a set date, or to the next, previous or current day, month, season or year. The sun, the seasons, the year and wine aging move together.
- **Fixed:** The last Spice of Life milestone gave no speed bonus. It now gives 10 % movement speed. Worlds created before this keep the old milestone.
- **Fixed:** The calendar's first year lasted until the second Early Spring. Year 2 now starts at the first Early Spring.

## Vine Shearing <!-- icon: minecraft:shears -->

- **New:** Shears stop Floral Enchantment's flower vines and Botania's solid vines growing, as Quark does for Vines. The same vine hanging below is removed and the sheared block shows a trimmed bottom.
- **New:** Shears on the tip of Weeping Vines, Twisting Vines, Cave Vines or Kelp stop it growing longer. A Cave Vines tip with Glow Berries is picked instead.
- **New:** The look-at tooltip shows "Sheared, stops growing" on sheared vines and sheared jungle grape vines, and "Stopped growing" on vine and kelp tips and Ivy that no longer grow.

## Watering Can <!-- icon: hearthandharvest:watering_can -->

- **New:** Watering Can (from Hearth and Harvest): 5 Copper Ingots around a Bucket. Holds 16 water from a water source and 16 Bone Meal loaded from the off hand.
- **New:** Used on a crop, the Watering Can spends 1 water and 1 Bone Meal for bone meal on the 3x3 around it. It puts out fire and lit campfires for 1 water.
- **New:** Used on a crop or soil, the Watering Can also soaks the farmland, Rich Soil farmland and Fertile Soil in the 3x3. Without Bone Meal, or with nothing to grow, soaking alone costs 1 water.

## Structures on Water and Slopes

- **Changed:** A structure no longer generates where its footprint holds more water than ground [over 1.5 times as much water as ground]; fewer than 9 water blocks under it never count.
- **Changed:** The water check covers every surface structure, also those without a dirt or sand base, such as desert pyramids, tent camps and wooden ruins. Swamp huts, witch huts and mangrove pillager towers still stand in water.
- **Changed:** Villages and other multi-part structures, such as camps, outposts and ruins, at a lake or river lose only the parts standing over water instead of not generating.
- **Changed:** Structure clusters (camps, ruin scatters, farm clusters) no longer place their outer structures on beaches, shores or rivers; the outer structures that fit still generate around the main one. Ships, fleets and wrecks are unchanged.
- **Changed:** An outer structure over too much water or air is left out instead of the whole cluster; the main and outer structures of a cluster first lose only their parts over water, and a village in a cluster its houses over cliffs.
- **Changed:** Structures set into the ground, such as Campsite camps, no longer generate where the ground under them is too far down to fill.
- **Changed:** Philip's Ruins ancient crypt entrances sit level with the ground instead of in a pit.
- **Changed:** Undead castles generate on uneven ground more often.
- **Changed:** Farmer's Structures fish ponds, the swamp fisherman house and Philip's Ruins level-three ruins sit on slopes without a square flat pad or a cut in the hillside around them; the carp pond's rim is level with the ground.
- **Changed:** Terralith spires no longer cut a square notch into the hills around their base.

- **New:** Some sky villages have 2 to 5 floating islands around them.

- **New:** Each structure in a cluster spawns its own mobs on its own ground only.

## Pillager Structures

- **Removed:** Pillager spawners in pillager structures. The Mysticrift End tower has no pillagers for now [3 spawners].
- **New:** Every pillager structure respawns its own illager mix, also inside clusters. Pillagers spawn day and night, the others only in the dark.
- **New:** Forts, Bastilles, Woodland Mansions and great tower and fort sieges: 35% pillagers, 5% ravagers, 15% each vindicators, evokers, illusioners and witches.
- **New:** Mysticrift towers and tower sieges: the same mix without ravagers.
- **Changed:** Other outpost towers and lone pillager camps: 75% pillagers, 20% vindicators, 5% witches [pillagers only].
- **New:** Pillager camp clusters: 60% pillagers, the rest vindicators, witches and evokers in equal shares.
- **New:** Pillager ships and fleets, and Unwrecked Ships with a pillager crew: 50% pillagers, the rest vindicators, evokers and witches in equal shares.
- **New:** Pillager caravans: pillagers, illusioners and vindicators in equal shares. Lone siege weapons and loot carriages: half pillagers, half vindicators.
- **New:** Witch wagons and Terralith's underground witch hut respawn witches, like the other witch huts.
- **Changed:** Evokers that respawn in a structure drop no Totem of Undying.

## World Generation

- **Changed:** Overworld structures generate plain lanterns and torches in place of soul lanterns and soul torches. Ancient cities and sculk dungeons keep theirs.
- **Changed:** YUNG's Better Dungeons no longer generate soul lanterns, soul campfires or soul sand.
- **Changed:** Crystal Peaks and Void Barrens are more common in the End [0.6 % and 1.8 % of the End].
- **Changed:** Distant Horizons' cut-off of deep terrain applies to the Overworld only, not the Nether and the End.
- **Fixed:** Sky Structures floating islands spawned under the sea and as two overlapping copies; they now float 16 to 79 blocks above the ground or the sea surface.
- **Fixed:** Underground cabins and Terralith cave structures spawned just under sea and lake floors and opened a hole in the seabed.
- **Changed:** Geodes that reach the sea floor are covered in the sea floor's own sand, gravel or clay instead of basalt and calcite; only their opening shows the crystals. Prismarine geodes are unchanged.
- **Changed:** Structures sharing biomes get even chances to generate unless a rate was set on purpose. Swamp pillager outposts, fake trader camps and Philip's ocean fortresses generate about 6 to 8 times as often; flower houses, country houses and warm ocean ruins about 3 to 6 times less often.
- **Changed:** Fortified villages have ruins or camps around them more often [20 % each].
- **Changed:** Structure searches (locate, maps) give up after 300 seconds [120].
- **New:** Sky villages generate 8 more houses: 3 Farmer's Delight farms, 3 vineyards with Vinery presses and barrels, and 2 taverns with Brewin' and Chewin' kegs and grape trellises.
- **Changed:** Firefly bushes also generate in savannas and shrublands, brushlands and all swamps [river and swamp only].
