# 1.1.04
Released 2026-10-29

## Salt <!-- icon: vintagedelight:salt_bricks -->

- **Changed:** One salt: Salt is now Vintage Delight's Salt Dust, pink [two salts].
- **Removed:** Salt Bucket.
- **Changed:** One Salt Block, pink, from 9 salt [two Salt Blocks].
- **Changed:** Rock salt ores, raw rock salt, salt clusters and buds, salt in cauldrons and the salted food sparkle are pink.
- **Changed:** The Salt's Salt Lamp renamed Large Salt Lamp. Recipe: 7 Salt Blocks, a Glowstone and Deepslate Tiles [raw rock salt block, torch, wooden slab].
- **Changed:** Beach salt patches have Raw Rock Salt Blocks under the layers [Salt Block].
- **Changed:** Salt Layers have the Salt Block's texture.
- **Changed:** Salt Blocks no longer grow Salt Layers next to water; the Evaporator still does.
- **Changed:** Salt Layers, salt bricks, smooth salt, salt pillars, salt lamps and the Evaporator melt touching ice and snow, like Salt Blocks.
- **Changed:** Salt Layers dissolve in water, like Salt Blocks.
- **Changed:** Salt no longer dissolves in rain [Salt Blocks and ores, 15% per random tick].
- **Changed:** Raw Rock Salt Blocks no longer dissolve in water.
- **Changed:** Salt no longer melts ice or snow when used on it or dispensed.

## JEI <!-- icon: minecraft:written_book -->

- **Changed:** The JEI item list opens with all equipment, grouped by material from wood to dragon, then other sets; each group lists tools, armor, then shields, horse armor and the like. Vanilla and modded tools, knives, arrows, guns and hats follow [copper and iron tools only].
- **Changed:** JEI world tabs draw mobs instead of their spawn eggs, and blocks that have no item (Frosted Ice, candle cakes, crops, dyed Create sails) as the block [spawn eggs and Barrier icons].
- **Changed:** JEI Jar Storage and Jar Fluids show one page per potion, the jar in the potion's colour [one page, Swiftness].
- **Changed:** JEI Stripping shows one log per page.
- **Changed:** JEI Block Dyeing and Soap Washing list every block the item works on.
- **Changed:** Fluid gauges in JEI fill on a log scale: small amounts show, and 250, 500 and 1000 mB look different.
- **Changed:** JEI tab Lava Generation renamed Lava Interactions.
- **Changed:** JEI drop tabs say "Cut with a knife" and "Cut with shears" [Broken with].
- **Changed:** The Jar Storage tab icon is a jar with a faucet.
- **Changed:** Tipped Arrows, Potion Buckets and Seasoning tabs use the same arrow as the other pack tabs.
- **Fixed:** Notes on JEI world tabs were cut off after three lines (Burning and others).
- **Fixed:** Potion Bundles did not show on JEI effect pages.

## Bread

- **Changed:** Any bread works in recipes that ask for bread: Garlic, Banana and Cantaloupe Bread, Bread Slice and Toast count. Honey, Mulberry, Glow and Jelly Bread (filled) do not.
- **Changed:** Roast Chicken, Caesar Salad, Crawling Sandwich, Guardian Burger and Sandwich, Turtle Egg Sandwich, Sweet Roll and the Create Factory rolls take any bread [plain Bread].
- **Changed:** Garlic Bread and Bread Slice crafts need a plain loaf.
- **Changed:** Caviar Toast is made from Toast [Bread].
- **Changed:** Sourdough Bread cuts into 3 Bread with a knife.
- **Changed:** Bread, Glow Bread, Banana Bread, Adzuki Bun, Strawberry Scones, Strange Eclair, Sweet Potato Dough and Batter take Wheat Flour [Wheat].
- **New:** Create, Sweet Potato and Oat Dough bake into Bread, and Sourdough into Sourdough Bread, in the Oven on a Baking Sheet: 1, 3 or 6 at a time.

## Pies & Cookies

- **Changed:** Bat Cookie, Chorus Cookie, Maple Cookie, Gingerbread Man and Hostile Delight's seven cookies are baked in the Oven: Cookie Dough and 2 of their flavour make 12 [crafting or furnace, 8].
- **New:** Every cookie fits in a jar.
- **Changed:** Rotten Pie, Green Pie and Chorus Flower Pie are baked in the Oven on a Pie Crust [crafting].
- **Changed:** Chorus Fruit Pie is baked in the Oven: Pie Crust, 2 Chorus Fruit Grain and 2 sugar [crafting, Wheat].
- **Changed:** Sponge Cake renamed Sponge Pie.
- **Changed:** Sponge Pie is baked in the Oven: Pie Crust, a Sponge, a Prismarine Shard and 2 sugar [crafting].
- **Changed:** Zucchini Quiche has a sequenced assembly on a Pie Crust.

## Cantaloupe

- **Changed:** Hamimelon renamed Cantaloupe: the melon block, stem, seeds, jam, jello, juice and shaved ice. Its slice is the Cantaloupe Slice [Hamimelon Slice].
- **Changed:** Delightful's Cantaloupe renamed Mini Cantaloupe and its seeds Mini Cantaloupe Seeds. 4 Cantaloupe Slices craft a Mini Cantaloupe.
- **Changed:** A Cantaloupe Slice crafts into Cantaloupe Seeds [Mini Cantaloupe Seeds].
- **Changed:** Cantaloupe Seeds and Mini Cantaloupe Seeds craft into each other.
- **Removed:** Hamimelon Popsicle and Canned Hamimelon. Existing ones become the Cantaloupe Popsicle and Canned Cantaloupe.
- **Changed:** Wild Mini Cantaloupes grow in deserts and badlands, where wild Cantaloupes grow [savannas, badlands plateaus and other warm dry grassland].

## Comfort and Nourishment

- **Changed:** Comfort and Nourishment on bowls, jars, shulker bowls and copper cups follow the recipe [the dish type].
  - Nourishment: 4 or more ingredients worth 8 hunger or more, on a food that restores 10 or more.
  - Comfort: 3 or more ingredients worth 5 hunger or more, or any of these foods that restores 10 or more.
  - Length: 4:00 from 10 hunger, 2:00 below, 1:00 for Comfort at 7 hunger or less.
- **Changed:** Golden foods give Nourishment 2:00, enchanted golden foods Nourishment 4:00.
- **Changed:** Comfort at 7 hunger or less lasts 1:00 on every food.
- **Removed:** Nourishment from all other foods (sandwiches, burgers, bars, pickles, pizza, cake slices, tankard drinks).
- **Removed:** Comfort and Nourishment from bottled foods.

## Cardboard Boxes

- **Changed:** Wares cardboard boxes and packages are brown like Dustrial Decor cardboard [pale yellow].
- **Changed:** Design and Decor's Cardboard Box renamed Old Cardboard Box, made by washing a Dustrial Decor Cardboard Box [crafted].
- **New:** Weathered Cardboard Box, made by washing an Old Cardboard Box. Same hardness and blast resistance as the Dustrial box; walks and sinks like it.

## Bell Peppers

- **New:** Stuffed Bell Peppers of every colour can also be cooked from a Roasted Bell Pepper, rice and a filling.
- **Fixed:** Coffee, Bell Pepper, Pale Bell Pepper and Dark Bell Pepper crops showed a missing texture at every stage.
- **Fixed:** The platter under the Bell Pepper Medley, Pale Bell Pepper Medley and Dark Bell Pepper Medley showed a missing texture.

## Look-at Tooltip

- **New:** The look-at tooltip shows "Needs Silk Touch" on blocks that only drop themselves with Silk Touch (glass, ice, Grass Blocks, ores, Stone), unless the held tool has Silk Touch.
- **New:** The look-at tooltip on a crop lists its fertile seasons, and says in red when it is out of season where it is planted (none under greenhouse glass).
- **Changed:** Operators see Barriers, Structure Voids and Light blocks in the look-at tooltip [hidden from everyone].

## Mob Spawn Caps

- **Changed:** When players are spread out, fewer mobs spawn: the hostile cap drops to 50 per player area above about 1.5 areas of loaded spawning chunks and to 25 above about 3 [70]. Bats, fish and axolotls drop by the same share; animals are unchanged.
- **Changed:** At those levels, mobs spawn only within 6 or 5 chunks of a player [8], and hostiles within 64 or 32 blocks above or below the nearest player [no limit].

## Phantom Bane

- **Removed:** Strong Potion of Phantom Bane (glowstone). Phantom Bane has no levels, like Night Vision. Existing strong potions become Uncraftable Potions.
- **Changed:** The full echo armor set gives Night Vision and Phantom Bane without a level [II].

## Food & Cooking <!-- icon: vintagedelight:fermenting_jar -->

- **Changed:** Sweet Potato Cupcake also bakes in the Oven (4) and the Cooking Pot (2).
- **Changed:** Honey Cake takes a Honey Bottle and a Honeycomb [2 Honey Bottles].
- **Changed:** Gnocchi and Cucumber Noodles work in every pasta recipe [Raw Pasta only].
- **New:** Overnight Oats crafting recipe: 3 Raw Oats, a fruit slice, a berry, milk and a bowl.
- **New:** Meat cultures grow in the Cooking Pot: a culture, Bone Meal and Cooking Oil make 3 cultures.
- **Changed:** Coleslaw restores 11 hunger [8].
- **Changed:** Fermented Garlic Honey gives Regeneration II for 5 s and Health Boost II for 30 s [Regeneration I and Health Boost I, 5 s].
- **Changed:** A Shulker Shell crafts 12 Shulker Bowls [8].
- **Fixed:** The white box on top of a two-high Fridge showed a missing texture.

## Brewing & Drinks <!-- icon: minecraft:brewing_stand -->

- **Removed:** Worlds last played before 1.0.7 no longer convert old Brewin' and Chewin' and Vinery blocks, keg, coaster and barrel contents, wine ages and effects.
- **Changed:** The Wandering Winemaker's spawn timer starts over once in existing worlds.

## Farming & Nature <!-- icon: vintagedelight:oat -->

- **New:** Lush Grass Blocks and Grass Slabs can be sheared; sheared, they stop spreading, as sheared Grass Blocks and Mycelium do. The look-at tooltip shows "Sheared, prevents spreading and converting to dirt" on all of them.
- **New:** Upgrade Aquatic corals grow fans and coral around their blocks under water, as vanilla corals do. Prismarine coral grows in any ocean, the others in warm oceans; Elder Prismarine coral does not grow.

## Blocks & Building

- **Changed:** Liquid Concrete sets after 30 s [32 s].
- **Removed:** Immersive Posts posts and trusses of nether brick, gold, lead, silver, constantan, electrum, uranium, concrete and leaded concrete. Wood, aluminium, steel, iron, copper and nickel remain. Placed posts of the removed materials disappear.
- **Removed:** Every Compat blocks for Create: Dreams & Desires rose, rubber and spirit wood, Upgrade Aquatic river wood, and all Lightman's Currency wood variants (263 blocks). Existing placed copies are lost.
- **Fixed:** Falling blocks such as anvils, sand and gravel did not splash when they entered water.

## Gear & Combat

- **Changed:** Felsic Sword durability 2501, like the other felsic tools [2502].
- **Changed:** Armor trims accept only Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Netherite, Echo, Felsic and Dragon Scale materials [also Emerald, Amethyst, Redstone, Lapis, Quartz, Zinc, Rose Quartz and Botania materials].
- **Fixed:** Players joining a world for the first time got no starter kit.
- **Fixed:** Dropped items with Curse of Ascension did not rise, and dropped items with Curse of Fleeting did not push nearby entities away.

## Mobs & Animals <!-- icon: miners_delight:arthropod -->

- **Fixed:** The Sunbloom's sunflowers showed their back.

## Interface & Tooltips

- **Fixed:** Syrup Coffee, Pumpkin Coffee, Cherry Blossom Coffee, Honey Coffee, Milk Coffee and Chocolate Coffee showed no tooltip.

## World Generation <!-- icon: minecraft:amethyst_cluster -->

- **Changed:** Tectonic cliff faces in new chunks sit slightly differently; a small step can show where new chunks meet older ones at a cliff.
- **Changed:** Structures by water, cliffs and drops are checked against the ground every 4 blocks [2]; a few starts at shorelines and edges come out differently.
- **Changed:** Sand, gravel and other falling blocks left floating by world generation stay in place until a block next to them changes [fell when the chunk first loaded].
- **Fixed:** `/locate` and explorer maps sometimes skipped the nearest Old Refinery or Mining Outpost.
- **Fixed:** Water in newly explored caves dropped moss carpet, seeds, azalea, glow berries, torches and other plants as items when it first flowed. The water still breaks them, without items.

## Performance & Stability <!-- icon: minecraft:clock -->

- **Changed:** New chunks generate faster.
- **Changed:** Chunks build their meshes faster on the client: seasonal grass and leaf colours are worked out once per biome.
- **Changed:** Loading a world, starting a server and reloading data spend less time swapping merged items in recipes.
- **Changed:** Stronghold positions are saved with the world and read back on later starts. Opening a world and starting a server take about 12 seconds less after the first time.
- **Changed:** WorldEdit no longer walks every block state when a world opens or a server starts, about 13 seconds less.
- **Changed:** The tuned biome layout is saved and read back while its settings are unchanged. The first world opened or server started after a launch takes about 10 seconds less.
- **Changed:** Distant Horizons uses 1 thread [3] and asks the server for chunks up to 256 chunks out [4096] by default.
- **Changed:** Immersive Weathering's growth checks on random ticks use less server time.
- **Changed:** Bees below y 32 stop moving about while no player is within 32 blocks, and carry on when one comes near.
- **Fixed:** Joining a world spent about a minute building the JEI Armor Trimming page.
- **Fixed:** The game crashed a few seconds into rain on the server. Pretty Rain is on the server too and can be turned back on.

## Mods Added & Removed

- **Removed:** Connected Textures Mod. Game start and resource reload are about 20 seconds shorter. The Pneumatic pressure chamber window and the FTB Quests open barrier no longer connect to neighbours.
