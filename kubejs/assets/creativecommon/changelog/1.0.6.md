# 1.0.6
Released 2026-10-14

All changes since 1.0.4.

## Seasons <!-- icon: minecraft:snowball -->

- **New:** Season types, shown on the season HUD. Arid (15 sand and terracotta biomes): no rain, summer crops all year [deserts and badlands rained].
- **New:** Frozen biomes (24 cold biomes and the frozen oceans): snow all year, winter crops only.
- **New:** Dry biomes (savannas, hot grasslands) rain in Mid Wet only. Tropical biomes (jungles, swamps, warm oceans) rain in every season but Mid Dry.
- **New:** Cold biomes (taigas, windswept and highland biomes, cold oceans): snow from Late Autumn to Early Spring.
- **Changed:** Below y 320, height alone no longer turns rain into snow. At y 320 and above, temperate and cold biomes always snow.
- **Changed:** Rain particles and clouds at y 350 [191]. Distant Horizons clouds follow.

## Mooblooms <!-- icon: minecraft:poppy -->

- **New:** More Mooblooms: 11 moobloom variants, each growing its own flowers, sheared off in two stages.
- **New:** Two different moobloom variants can breed a calf of a mixed variant.
- **Changed:** Cows in flower biomes spawn as mooblooms matching the biome's flowers. Azaleablooms spawn on moss in lush caves, rare witherblooms in soul sand valleys.
- **Removed:** Buzzier Bees' moobloom, Buttercup and Sunny.

## Shields <!-- icon: minecraft:shield -->

- **Changed:** Axes held by mobs disable shields too.
- **New:** Every shield tier takes a banner in the crafting grid.
- **Changed:** Fire, campfires, magma and stoves are blocked only by netherite shields and above. Cactus, berry bushes and other thorns only by diamond and above.

## Cans

- **New:** Cans for cooked foods, bread, salads, soups and golden foods, then for raw fruit, raw fish, cookies, pastas, pie fillings and feasts.
- **Removed:** 11 raw cans, including mint, acorn, grapes and several raw meats.
- **Changed:** Cactus Chunk renamed Cactus Flesh, its can Canned Cactus Flesh. White Strawberries have their own can.

## World Generation <!-- icon: minecraft:amethyst_cluster -->

- **New:** Wild flowers by biome, including Botania flowers, and flower patches in biomes that had none. New chunks only.
- **Fixed:** Wild plants, bushes and trees did not generate on Immersive Weathering soils. New chunks only.
- **New:** Structure groups: villages with towers, ruin clusters, sunken ruins, pillager forts and camps with siege weapons, a pillager fleet, driftwood flotillas. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** Towers generate in every biome their wood belongs to, Terralith biomes included. New chunks only.
- **Changed:** The ground under structures is filled in or shaped to the terrain, and water structures that would flood dry land are not placed. New chunks only.

## Mobs & Animals <!-- icon: minecraft:squid_spawn_egg -->

- **Changed:** Animal spawns per biome: flying birds, chickens on shores, ducks and geese by ponds. Quark's Shiba no longer spawns.
- **New:** Duck and goose nests and egg crates for Untitled Duck Mod.

## Interface & Tooltips <!-- icon: minecraft:paper -->

- **Changed:** JEI lists the recipes you can craft first.
- **New:** WTHIT shows the growth of 62 more plants.
- **New:** Placeable foods say so in their tooltip ("Placeable", "while sneaking", "on plate").
- **Fixed:** The Legendary Tooltips underline crossed the item model, and long container titles overflowed their plaque.

## Food & Cooking <!-- icon: farmersdelight:cooking_pot -->

- **Changed:** Stack sizes set by kind for over 1000 items: golden foods 16; bowl meals, soups, salads and drinks 32; filled buckets, cakes and feasts 8; eggs 64.

## Performance & Stability <!-- icon: minecraft:clock -->

- **Fixed:** On the server, planting magic beans, harvesting crops with a hoe, tree growth or WorldEdit edits left ghost blocks.

## Mods Added & Removed

- **New:** Paintings: Atmospheric Paintings Pack, Dark Paintings, Dimensional Paintings, MC style paintings, REBIRTH: Paintings, Too Many Paintings!, Cycle Paintings, Just Enough Painting Previews. Every variant is also an Immersive Paintings painting.
- **New:** Subterrestrial, Underground Bunkers, Volcanic Caverns, Campsite Structures.
- **New:** Witch Mobility, Firefly Bush, Fortune on Netherite, The Splash Milk, Sticky It!, Bottled Air, Unlit Campfire, Rain Should Extinguish Campfires, Breedable Killer Rabbit.
- **New:** Cool Rain Reforged, Pretty Rain, Lava Clear View, Neko's Enchanted Books, MEED.
