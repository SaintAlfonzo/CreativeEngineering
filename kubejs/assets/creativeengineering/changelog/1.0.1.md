# 1.0.1
Released 2026-09-28

All changes since 1.0.0.

## World Generation <!-- icon: minecraft:amethyst_cluster -->

- **New:** 44 small structures: villager houses and workshops, tents and camps, watchtowers, stables, forest ruins, churches, statues, illager siege weapons and ships, sky villages. New chunks only.
- **New:** Felsic Block veins in the End Stone of the outer End biomes. Needs a Netherite or Felsic Pickaxe; drops 2 to 4 Felsic Scrap, or the block with Silk Touch. New chunks only.

## Performance & Stability <!-- icon: minecraft:clock -->

- **Fixed:** Chest loot could roll an explorer map to a structure that never generates, which froze the server.

## Mods Added & Removed

- **New:** Food and farming: Abnormals Delight, Berry Good, Canned Goods, Golden Foods, Neapolitan, The Salt, The Dirty Stuff, Ice Prevents Crop Growth.
- **New:** Structures: Blocky Siege, Greek Villager House, Hobbit House, Hopo Better Underwater Ruins, It Takes a Pillage, Old Fisherman Swamp House, Revamped Shipwrecks, Sawmill House, Simply Houses, Unwrecked Ships, Villages & Pillages.
- **New:** Boats and carts: AstikorCarts, Boatload, Boat Item View, BoatView360, Ice Boat Nerf.
- **New:** Alex's Abnormalities, Breezy, Bushier Flowers, Colored Water, Create: Fast Schematic Cannon, Floral Enchantment, Glow Ink Plus, Glowing Illagers, Make Bubbles Pop, No Hostiles Around Campfire, Rare Ice, Re:Deco, Shulker Box Slot, Starter Kit, Tinted Campfires, Upgrade Aquatic, XP Tome.
