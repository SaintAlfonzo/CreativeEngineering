# 1.0.8
Released 2026-10-19

All changes since 1.0.7.

## Time and Seasons <!-- icon: minecraft:clock -->

- **Changed:** A day lasts 45 minutes of daylight and 15 of night (was 98 and about 8).
- **Changed:** Seasons count world days: a sub-season lasts 8 days, a season 24. The day changes at sunrise.
- **Changed:** On a server, the sun, the seasons and wine aging stop while no player is online.
- **New:** SeasonHUD shows the day out of the season's total and the time of day. The Serene Seasons Calendar tooltip shows season, day, year, time of day and the next sub-season.

## Baking <!-- icon: minecraft:cake -->

- **New:** Muffins, cookies, tarts, pies and cakes bake in the Oven, each in its bakeware. Muffins come out on paper. The other recipes stay.
- **New:** Baking Sheet, Muffin Tin, Pie Tin and Cake Pan: an Iron Plate hammered into each in turn with the Engineer's Hammer, on a Cutting Board or in the crafting grid.
- **Fixed:** The Engineer's Hammer turned a Cutting Board instead of cutting on it.
- **New:** Cornmeal, from corn and corn kernels in every crusher. Used in tortillas and cornbread batter, and as flour in pancakes, pies and cakes.
- **New:** Pie Crust from 3 flour and milk. Cake base also bakes in the Oven.

## Brewin' and Chewin' <!-- icon: brewinandchewin:keg -->

- **New:** Keg fluid slots: a filled container in the upper one empties into the tank, the empty container comes out below. JEI's [+] also fills the tank from containers in your inventory.
- **Changed:** Pipes fill a Keg only with fluids its recipes use, and drain it only while it is not fermenting.
- **Changed:** Steel Toe Stout gives Resistance II for 90 seconds (was Resistance I for 60).
- **Changed:** Fermenting: Beer takes a dandelion (was wheat seeds), Mead a red mushroom (was wheat seeds), Bloody Mary salt (was sweet berries), Steel Toe Stout any metal dust and blaze powder (was iron ingot and wheat).
- **New:** Create Spout and Item Drain fill and empty tankards of every drink, 250 mB each.
- **New:** Quiche and Ham and Cheese Sandwich by sequenced assembly. Pizza takes dough (was 3 wheat).
- **Removed:** Sweet Berry Jam, Glow Berry Marmalade and Apple Jelly.

## Vinery <!-- icon: minecraft:glass_bottle -->

- **Changed:** Wine ages in months, one per sub-season (was years). Wine stored out of sky light ages 2.5 times as fast (was 1.5).
- **Changed:** The Vinery banner gives Health Boost II for 60 seconds within 8 blocks (was Regeneration II).
- **New:** Vinery banner recipe: a banner, a Nether Star and 7 wines.
- **Changed:** Fermentation Barrel renamed Cask.
- **Fixed:** Placed Apple Wine held one bottle per block (holds 3). Praetorian did not fit wine racks.
- **Removed:** Vinery's Mead (Brewin' and Chewin' Mead stays), King Danis Wine and the Vinery calendar.

## World Generation <!-- icon: minecraft:amethyst_cluster -->

- **Changed:** Villages next to a town generate 64 to 96 blocks from it (was 112 to 160). New chunks only.
- **Fixed:** Superflat worlds generated normal terrain.

## Gameplay

- **Removed:** All advancements. Saved advancement progress is deleted.

## Interface & Tooltips <!-- icon: minecraft:written_book -->

- **New:** Food that can be placed on a Coaster says so in its tooltip.
