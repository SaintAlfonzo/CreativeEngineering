## Ore Processing & Metal Unification <!-- icon: create:crushed_raw_iron -->

- **Changed:** Ores follow one chain for copper, iron, gold, zinc, aluminum, lead, silver, nickel and uranium: ore or raw ore, then Crushed Raw ore, then Immersive Engineering dust, then ingot.
  - Create crushing an ore: 1 Crushed Raw ore + 50% chance of a second (was 75%), 75% Nugget of Experience, 12% Cobblestone. Deepslate ores: 2 + 25% chance of a third.
  - Immersive Engineering Crusher: ore gives 2 Crushed Raw ore, raw ore gives 1 + 33% chance of a second, raw ore block gives 12 (was dust in every case).
  - Immersive Engineering Crusher: Crushed Raw ore gives 1 dust + 33% chance of a second; a Crushed Raw ore pile gives 12 dust.
  - Arc Furnace: Crushed Raw ore gives 1 ingot + 50% chance of a second; a Crushed Raw ore pile gives 13 ingots + 50% chance of one more.
  - Crushed Raw ore piles smelt into the metal's storage block (90 s furnace, 45 s blast furnace).
- **Removed:** Create and Immersive Engineering ore crushing recipes for these 9 metals (replaced above), and crushing ores or raw ores with the Engineer's Hammer for copper, iron, gold, aluminum, lead, silver, nickel and uranium.
- **New:** 16 grit piles (storage blocks for dust, fall like sand, need a tool): Aluminum, Copper, Gold, Iron, Lead, Nickel, Silver, Uranium, Zinc, Diamond, Rose Gold, Cast Iron, Electrum, Steel, Constantan and Brass Grit Pile.
  - 9 dust or grit craft a pile and back. Each metal pile smelts into its storage block (90 s furnace, 45 s blast furnace).
- **New:** Zinc Grit, Brass Grit and Cast Iron Grit. Zinc Grit comes from crushing Crushed Raw Zinc and smelts, blasts or arc-smelts into a Zinc Ingot.
- **New:** Rose Gold Nugget and Rose Gold Rod. Rose gold nuggets, rods and Rose Gold Blocks count as gold in recipes.
- **Changed:** Copper nuggets are unified to Immersive Engineering's Copper Nugget. Create's and Everything Copper's copper nugget recipes are removed, and their recipes use and give the Immersive Engineering nugget.
- **Changed:** Immersive Engineering's Copper, Iron and Gold Plates replace Create's Copper, Iron and Gold Sheets in every recipe that used or made a sheet.
- **Changed:** Plates: Create's Mechanical Press presses copper, iron, gold and zinc ingots into Immersive Engineering plates. The Engineer's Hammer makes every plate, Zinc Plate included.
- **Removed:** Create's own sheet pressing, Create Compressed's sheet recipes, and Create Crafts & Additions and Vintage Improvements plate rolling for every metal.
- **New:** Zinc Plate, and plate blocks for 11 metals: Aluminum, Cast Iron, Constantan, Electrum, Lead, Netherite, Nickel, Silver, Steel, Uranium and Zinc Plate Block.
  - 9 plates craft a plate block and back (Copper Plate Block only breaks back into plates). Create Compressed's Copper, Iron, Gold and Brass sheet blocks are the Copper, Iron, Gold and Brass Plate Blocks.
- **Changed:** Rods: 1 ingot gives 4 rods on Create Crafts & Additions' Rolling Mill and on Immersive Engineering's Metal Press with the Rod Mold (was 2), for copper, iron, gold, zinc, aluminum, lead, silver, nickel and uranium. 2 Zinc Ingots craft 4 Zinc Rods.
- **New:** Iron, Gold, Zinc, Silver, Nickel, Brass and Constantan Wire.
- **Changed:** Wires: a plate and Engineer's Wire Cutters craft 2 wires (was 1); 1 plate gives 4 wires on the Rolling Mill and on the Metal Press with the Wire Mold (was 1 ingot for 2). Covers copper, iron, gold, zinc, aluminum, lead, silver and nickel.
- **Removed:** Immersive Engineering's ingot rod and wire pressing, Create Crafts & Additions ingot rolling, and Vintage Improvements rod and wire coiling for lead and uranium (replaced above).
- **Changed:** Immersive Posts' gold rod takes a Gold Ingot only. Create Crafts & Additions recipes use the pack's Gold Wire.
- **New:** Cast Iron Ingot in the Kiln: 1 Iron Ingot + Coke Dust or Petcoke Dust.
- **New:** Red Electrum Ingot (Project Red's red alloy): 1 Electrum Ingot + 8 Redstone crafted, + 4 Redstone in a heated Create mixer, or + 3 Redstone in the Arc Furnace. Project Red's red iron recipe is removed.
- **Removed:** Vintage Improvements recipes for vanadium, shadow steel and refined radiance parts, its netherite, andesite, cast iron and uranium wire and rod coiling, and its sulfur trioxide pressurizing.
