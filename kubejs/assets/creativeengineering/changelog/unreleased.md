## Raspberries <!-- icon: hearthandharvest:raspberry -->

- **New:** Raspberries (from Hearth and Harvest): wild Raspberry Bushes grow in temperate grassy biomes. Craft a Raspberry into Raspberry Pips to plant a bush on dirt or farmland.
- **New:** A Raspberry Bush is picked by hand only when fully grown, for 2-3 Raspberries. It slows anything walking through but never hurts. Foxes pick ripe bushes.
- **New:** Raspberry: 2 hunger, eaten fast. Grows best in spring.

## Watering Can <!-- icon: hearthandharvest:watering_can -->

- **Changed:** The Watering Can is now the Copper Watering Can: 8 water and 8 Bone Meal, works on 1 block [16 and 16, 3x3]
- **New:** Iron, Golden, Diamond, Netherite and Dragon Watering Cans, each upgraded from the tier below at a Smithing Table with an Iron Block, a Gold Block, a Diamond, a Netherite Ingot or a Dragon Scale. The can keeps its water and Bone Meal.
- **New:** Area and capacity of each: Iron 2x2 and 12, Golden 3x3 and 16, Diamond 4x4 and 20, Netherite 5x5 and 32, Dragon 7x7 and 64. On a 2x2 or 4x4 the extra row is on the side of the block aimed at.
- **New:** Watering Cans fill to full from a Well (1 bucket) or a water Cauldron (1 level; Diamond 2; Netherite and Dragon a full Cauldron). A Cauldron with fewer levels is emptied for a partial fill.
- **New:** Plants grown by a Watering Can show water splash particles to every nearby player
- **Changed:** Watering Cans do nothing to saplings, propagules, azaleas, nether fungi and mushrooms
