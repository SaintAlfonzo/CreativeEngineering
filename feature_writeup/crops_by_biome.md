# Crops by biome (generated)

Generated by `python3 tools/gen_crop_worldgen_table.py`. Do not edit by hand; the explanations, ground rules and gaps are in [crops.md](crops.md) sections 2.7 and 2.8. Rerun after adding or updating mods, after `/dumpbiomes`, after retuning biomes (`biome_config.json`) or after editing `wild_crops.json`.

Every table assumes the pack's placement (`kubejs/generation_scripts/worldgen/wild_crops.json`, CEConfig `[worldgen.systems] wildCrops = true`). Section 1 also shows each crop with the switch off (the mods' own spawns).

- **Rate**: patch attempts per chunk. "1 in 30 chunks" means a chunk tries once with probability 1/30.
- **Share**: the part of the dimension (post-tuning biome areas from `biome_config.json`) covered by the biomes the feature is added to. Ocean biomes (`#minecraft:is_ocean`, about 41% of the overworld) and underground biomes are left out: a surface attempt there lands on water or in another biome. "no area data" means the biomes have no sampled area (skylands, caves).
- **Per 1000 chunks**: rate x share x 1000. An upper bound: an attempt still fails when the ground, water or space test fails (wild cabbages need sand or dirt at the spot).
- **Height**: where the attempt is placed. "surface" means the top block of the column (heightmap).
