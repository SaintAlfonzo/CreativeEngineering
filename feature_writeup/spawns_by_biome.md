- **World share** = share weighted by biome area (`biome_config.json`): the part of the category's spawn picks across the region that go to this mob, assuming equal attempts per area. - Biomes in no dimension (unused or dimension-less) are left out. Area `-` = no sampled area (sky dimensions, pocket dimensions); `<0.03` = under the climate sample's resolution.
