# Crop growth seasons audit

Generated by `tools/crop_season_audit.py` (`/cegen run crop_season_audit`) on 2026-10-30 from the dumps in `kubejs/exported/`, `kubejs/generation_scripts/worldgen/crop_seasons.json` and the jars in `mods/`. Do not edit by hand; the design is in [crops.md](crops.md) section 2.12.1.

Crop seasons (patcherMod `seasons/CropSeasons`): each season is fertile (growth +25 %), neutral or off (growth -25 %) for the crop's type, from the biome's calendar (four seasons, or wet/dry in tropical, dry and arid biomes). Only growth that fires Forge's `onCropsGrowPre` is checked, so a plant without the event grows at its own speed whatever its type. Serene Seasons' own season tags (column SS) are inert: fertility.toml `seasonal_crops = false`.
