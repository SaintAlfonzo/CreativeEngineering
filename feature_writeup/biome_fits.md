**How the plan is built.**

- **One flat set of fits, `land/<family>`,** all children of an empty `land` parent (as the empty `tower` parent
  works now). A structure tagged with several families gets all of their biomes. There are two kinds of family:
  - **Biome-type families** (plains, highlands, forest, taiga, snowy, desert, badlands, savanna, swamp, beach...) say
    what a biome looks like. Villages, houses, ruins and dungeons use these.
  - **Wood families** say where a wood belongs. They are the 3.17 tower lists unchanged, renamed: `tower/oak` ->
    `land/oak`, `tower/spruce` -> `land/spruce`, `tower/acacia` -> `land/acacia`, `tower/mangrove` -> `land/mangrove`,
    `tower/cherry` -> `land/cherry`, `tower/desert` -> `land/sand` (sand deserts and sand beaches), `tower/end` ->
    `land/end`. Each still holds the trees' biomes plus the open land its climate gets (the 3.17 user rules).
  - Three lists serve both uses: `land/birch` = `tower/birch`, `land/dark_forest` = `tower/dark_oak`, and
    `land/jungle` = `tower/jungle`. To match exactly, `land/birch` takes blooming valley, sakura grove and valley,
    orchid swamp and snowy shield, and `land/dark_forest` takes lavender valley and Siberian grove.
  - Other wooden structures can move onto wood families later (the backlog's wood-matching line).
- **Each structure goes to the families its look and its mod's own list point to.** A desert village gets every sand
  biome; a spruce-and-stone villager house gets temperate open land, highlands and taiga. The "Built from" column is
  the block count over every template the structure can place, as in 3.17.
- **Every land biome is in at least one family.** The 0% biomes (they exist but never generate in this layout) are
  listed too, as in the tower fits, so a later biome-tuning change leaves no hole. Skylands stay out of every family, as
  everywhere else. Peaks and mushroom fields are only used where the mod already had them, or for sky and underground
  structures.
- **Overlaps are on purpose,** for variety, as with the towers. Where two village styles share a biome they share its
  slots, so the group grid still caps the total there.
- **Sky and underground structures** get every land biome (`land/any`). The sky village also gets the oceans
  (`land/sky` = `land/any` + `#minecraft:is_ocean`), for the backlog line "ensure settlements can spawn above land or
  water". The water rule already lets sky structures into oceans.
