2026-10-03: the user asked for the three "Planned Changes" in feature_writeup/structure_tuning.md (now sections 3.12 and 3.13, tests ST-49..55).

- **Earth fill:** `ChunkGeneratorEarthFillMixin` = @ModifyVariable STORE index 15 (the StructureStart local in `tryGenerateStructure` m_223104_, verified against SRG bytecode) -> `EarthFill.filter` returns `StructureStart.INVALID_START` on a cancel. Counts template earth (block tag `creativeengineering:earth_fill/earth`) in the bottom `baseLayers` (3) layers of PoolElement/Template pieces, skipping terrain_matching pieces, against `ChunkGenerator#getBaseColumn` water/air. Limits `maxOverWater` 24 / `maxOverAir` 256 (-1 unlimited), `overrides` list, exempt structure tag (sky, ships, seabed). Not estimated: Beardifier fill under terrain-adapted structures. `/place`, `/cdevplace`, `/locate` don't hit it.
- **Defaults came from a simulation, not the game:** `tools/earth_fill_sim.sh` (EarthFillSim, builtin registries + client-extra.jar + a classpath ResourceManager; Forge's VanillaPackResources needs FML) generated vanilla villages/outposts/igloo/mansion: villages hang a median 60-150 earth blocks in air, 0 in water. Small modded ruins with pads were NOT simulated; calibrate with `log = ALL` (ST-52).
- **Banks:** Monobank vault = house piece + straight-street piece (both weight 15); tags `monobank/vault_houses` x0.18 and `monobank/vault_streets` x0.16 in pool_tags.json give ~5x (pool shrinks, so not x0.2). Scale only the 10 injected pieces: sub-pools (12/3/1) would round.
- **Driftwood:** the 5 UA placed features now require water below (`block_predicate_filter` + `matching_fluids`, explicit fluid list; `#tag` only parses with RegistryOps); land driftwood = 3 new `creativeengineering:driftwood_land/*` features at 1/10 attempts. Headless parse: `PlacementModifier.CODEC` with `RegistryOps.create(JsonOps.INSTANCE, RegistryAccess.BUILTIN.get())`.
- **Not verified live:** the game (10 GB resident, 6 GB swapped) was running, so no scratch server was started. Jar built in patcherMod/build/libs (1.0.4). Next: when the game is closed, boot a scratch server (memory `scratch-server-verify`) to confirm the mixin applies, the pool report numbers and the driftwood data load.

- **7.3.1 (2026-10-13, 1.0.6 build/libs):** water/air under a bearded piece counts only past `beardReach` 6 (`buryReach` 10), gap measured through air+water to solid; mound columns (gap > `maxMoundHeight` 8, once per column); limits by the start's own earth count (`[worldgen.earth_fill.sizes]` bounds 150/600/2000, [air, mound] per size); `maxOverAir` removed; overrides `water,air[,mound]`; mansion exempt. Reach measured with `tools/earth_fill_sim.sh <seed> <n> beard` (vanilla Beardifier, public ctor in Forge). Deviation from plan: no size tags/tool (scan has no earth counts; jigsaw earth varies per start).
- **Pruning (2026-10-13, built, NOT scratch-tested by user request):** structures in `earth_fill/prune` (= `#creativeengineering:type/village`) drop non-root pieces over `pruneMaxHanging` 8 / `pruneMaxMound` 4 plus their jigsaw subtree, then the rest is judged. Tree = junctions: child's first junction at parent's jigsaw, parent's matching one adjacent with -deltaY, pieces listed parents-first (`EarthFill.links`); parent junction removed via field f_72602_. Metas not pruned (fall back to plain anchor). Sim: whole-village cancels 22-40% -> 5-12%.
- **Sim gotcha:** headless sims don't bind fluid tags; the old `FluidTags.WATER` test was always false there, so the 10-03 "0 over water" was wrong. Real: villages put house floors in the y-62 water layer; after the beard ~20-40% of vanilla villages still cancel on water deeper than 6 (user to decide in ST-52).

- **2026-10-19 footprint water share (structure_tuning.md 7.5, 1.0.9 in mods/ b82483aa, scratch-tested ST-199..205):** after the limits, each earth piece's box at its surface layer (minY + TerrainAdaptation.sink) counts WATER vs SOLID; cancel when water > ground*(1+`maxWaterSurplus` 0.5). User's wording "water converted vs solid replaced or left alone" read as: all footprint water (beard/earth fills it) vs all dry ground. Seabed/air neither; spots with > baseLayers terrain above (Terrain#top) neither: without that rule underground structures (Philip's ancient_dungeon, Subterrestrial cabins: seabed under the sea, aquifer water) were false cancels. Skipped for water_fill/structures. Inland census: 14 share-only cancels of 972 (lake villages, drum towers on water, windmill).
