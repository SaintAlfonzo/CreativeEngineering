## Shared findings

- **Jar machinery** (all patcherMod, see mixed_jars.md 3 and 5): `mixin/SupplementariesJarTileMixin` (one slot per
  capacity point, one item per slot), `compat/JarMixing` (`canMix`, families = tags `#creativeengineering:jar_family/<name>`
  from [jartags.js](../../kubejs/data/creative_delight/kubejs/creativeengineering/jar_tags.js), keys `i.t_jar_family_*`), the two renderer mixins
  (`SupplementariesJarRendererMixin`, `SupplementariesJarItemRendererMixin`), `SupplementariesJarItemTooltipMixin`,
  `compat/WthitHolderContents`. What a jar takes at all is `#supplementaries:cookies` (`i.t_jar_cookies`).
- **Capacity** is Supplementaries' `[functional.jar] capacity = 12` in `config/supplementaries-common.toml` ("leave at 12
  for pixel accuracy"); the item slot count follows it. Liquids use Moonlight soft fluids: JSON in
  `kubejs/data/creativeengineering/moonlight/soft_fluids/` (37 files, generated by `tools/gen_soup_soft_fluids.py`, plus
  hand-written drinks) with `containers[].capacity` (a bowl of soup 2) and `food.divider`.
- The JEI jar tabs read the capacity (`tab_drops.py`): changing it changes their rows after `/cegen run jei_world`.

## jars #1: Jar capacity rework
Status **Planned** / Work patcherMod / TODO `items/jars #1`

### Goal
Jar maximum 60, every other jar value scaled to the same ratios, for finer steps; raw fruit holds 36.

### Now
- Capacity 12; each unit moves the liquid level by 1 of 12 pixel rows. At 60 the level moves one row per 5 units, so a
  value under 5 shows no change on the jar: the reason the row says increments of 5.
- Every liquid amount is in capacity units: soft fluid `containers[].capacity` (soup 2, bottle 1 in the mods' own files),
  Supplementaries' own fluids in its jar, cookies one per slot (cap = slot count), mob capture.
- Raw fruit 36 means a per-family cap below the jar maximum (60 / 36 is not an integer cost per item).

### Build
1. Config `capacity = 60` (edit `config/supplementaries-common.toml` and copy to `configs_edited/root_config/config/`).
2. Scale every soft fluid: our 37 files by changing the generator (`tools/gen_soup_soft_fluids.py`: capacity x5) and the
   mods' own files by datapack overrides at the same paths (`data/<ns>/moonlight/soft_fluids/<name>.json`) in
   `kubejs/data/`; a generator pass for Supplementaries / Moonlight / mod fluids is needed (none exists: unverified count).
3. Family caps: `JarMixing` gets a `capOf(item)` (default 60, `raw_fruit` 36) used by `SupplementariesJarTileMixin`
   `canPlaceItem` and `isFull`; slots stay the maximum.
4. Comparator, WTHIT "(5 / 60)" and the tooltip read total / capacity already.

### Decisions
Mixed-item cost per family (60 cookies per jar feels large). Recommend a per-family cap map in config, defaults: 60 for
small items, 36 for raw fruit, redstone and the gem family (row 2).

### Gotchas
Existing worlds: jars saved at 12 keep 12-unit amounts and read as 1/5 full; needs a conversion on load (multiply when
the saved capacity is under the new one) or accept a one-off reset. Lowering the capacity drops layers on load
(mixed_jars.md 4).

### Tests (MJ-)
- MJ-15 (proposed): fill from a 12-era world jar: amounts scaled; soup bowl = 10 units, bottle = 5; raw fruit refuses the 37th.

## jars #2: New jar contents
Status **Planned** / Work patcherMod / resource pack / TODO `items/jars #2`

### Goal
Jars take all candy mixes, cotton candy, diamond, emerald, redstone (opaque red with black accents, 36, glows slightly;
glowstone and gunpowder the same), coal and charcoal.

### Now

  `berries`, `dried_fruit` (jartags.js). "All candy mixes" likely means one family for every candy item (candies,

### Build
1. New families in `jartags.js` with `i.` keys: `gems` (`i.diamond`, `i.emerald`, ...), `dusts` (redstone, glowstone dust,
   gunpowder), `fuels` (coal, charcoal), `candy_mix` merging the sweets; add `i.t_jar_family_*` keys in item_dict.
   Cotton candy item id unverified (grep `cotton`).
2. Render: a "pile" look for dusts and gems (opaque, tinted layers with black accents): extend the renderer mixins with a
   per-item layer style table, textures under `kubejs/assets/` or the patcherMod resources.
3. Glow: the placed jar's light level from its contents (an emissive layer plus `getLightEmission` on the block state); how
   Supplementaries lights a lava jar is unverified: read `JarBlock`.

### Decisions
Whether glowstone and gunpowder join the redstone cap (36). Recommend yes, matching the row.

3. Lava bottle: soft fluid json (generator) or a jar fluid row; check `kitchen #18` for the intended look.

### Decisions
Which items are family vs alone. Recommend: raw fruit and cooked fruit one family each; honeycomb, kelp, vanilla, tobacco,
seashells their own single-kind entries.

## jars #4: Soups and stews take 1 jar space
Status **Planned** / Work patcherMod / TODO `items/jars #4`

### Goal
A bowl of soup or stew fills 1 space of the jar (was 2); enchanted soups and stews glint in the jar.

### Now
- Generated soft fluids hold `"capacity": 2` for the bowl and `"food": {"divider": 2}` (e.g.
  `soft_fluids/acacia_blossom_soup.json`): 6 bowls per 12. The generator writes them (`tools/gen_soup_soft_fluids.py`);
  mixed_jars.md 9 describes it.
- Golden / Enchanted Golden soups share one fluid each (mixed_jars.md 9). Whether a soft fluid can glint in the jar render is
  unverified: the jar draws fluid as a tinted still texture; the glint needs a render hook in `SupplementariesJarRendererMixin`.

### Build
1. Generator: capacity 1 (after row 1: 5 of 60) and divider to match; regenerate (`/cegen run soup_soft_fluids`).
2. Keep pour-in and pour-out symmetric (in/out use the same capacity; mixed_jars.md 9).
3. Glint: tag enchanted soups (`#creativeengineering:enchanted_foods`?) and draw an animated glint layer over the liquid in
   both jar renderers; or drop it if too costly.

### Decisions
1 of 12 or 1 of 60 (row 1 scales every value; 1 of 12 is 5 of 60). Recommend: finish row 1 first, then set soups to 5.

## jars #6: Why are hyperxp and toast a jar family?
Status **Planned** (investigation) / Work none / TODO `items/jars #6`

### Build
Reword the row (name the items, or one row per loose item: "Toast: up to 12, one kind per jar") in `cookie_rows`; or give them
a neutral label in the generated notes. No tag change.

### Decisions
None; recommend one row per loose item once row 3 adds more single-kind items.
