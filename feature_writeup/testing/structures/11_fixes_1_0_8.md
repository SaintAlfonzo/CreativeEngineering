| 09 3.2: no flat world while `biomeLayout` is on | Cause traced in the 1.19.2 code: a world's dimensions are decoded with `RegistryCodecs.dataPackAwareCodec`, which runs `RegistryLoader.overrideRegistryFromResources` and lets every data pack's `dimension/overworld.json` replace the preset's overworld (ours, Terralith's, Tectonic's; on new and loaded worlds, server and single player alike). `RegistryLoaderPresetOverworldMixin` (on `m_206762_`): when the dimension registry's own overworld isn't a noise generator on a multi-noise biome source (flat, debug, single biome), it is put back after the data pack overrides, with one log line. Normal worlds are unchanged | `mixin/RegistryLoaderPresetOverworldMixin.java` (new), `creativeengineering.mixins.json` |
