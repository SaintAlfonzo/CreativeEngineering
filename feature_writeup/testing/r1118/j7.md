## Findings

- **SQ-HH-1269 (FAIL, real bug, high impact): no `glm_entries.json` global loot modifier is active in the pack.**
  Pitchfork on grass gave the normal grass drops (seeds), no straw (0 of 200 rolls). Cause: Creative Loot's
  `LootModifierManagerMixin` (`development/creativeLoot/src/main/java/com/SaintAlfonzo/creativeloot/mixin/LootModifierManagerMixin.java`)
  injects into `m_5787_(Ljava/util/Map;...)V`, but in Forge 1.19.2-43.3.13 `LootModifierManager` the real method is
  `apply(Ljava/util/Map;Lnet/minecraft/server/packs/resources/ResourceManager;Lnet/minecraft/util/profiling/ProfilerFiller;)V`
  and `m_5787_` is only the synthetic bridge with `(Ljava/lang/Object;...)` (javap of the universal jar). The injector
  matches nothing and, with no `defaultRequire`, fails silently: debug.log shows the mixin "Mixing ... into
  LootModifierManager", but no `[GlmMerge]` line is logged on either load. Since commit 89a25077 (2026-11-17, Rules
  fixes P3c) moved every own-jar Forge list to `data/<ns>/glm_entries.json`, all those modifiers are dropped: Hearth
  (`corn_husk`, `pitchfork_straw`), Brewin', Ends Delight, Rustic, Veggies, Vintage, Vinery, Exposure (main and expanded).
  Fix: target `apply(Ljava/util/Map;...)V` (remap=false), add `"injectors": {"defaultRequire": 1}` to
  `creativeloot.mixins.json` so a miss crashes instead; rerun SQ-HH-1269 and any loot-modifier rows of those members.
  TODO family: creative_mods (Creative Loot) or tooling/scratch-1118 follow-ups.
