   structure. Effect: those structures get no soft beard / trim. Fix idea: treat a namespace with a loaded datapack
   (`kubejs/data/<ns>` or any pack namespace) as present, or skip the absent filter for worldgen registries. Family:
   Structures and worldgen (terrain adaptation), code in Creative Common's tag helper. Needs a `:creativeCommon:` rebuild.
