# Creative Compat

**Compatibility fixes and tweaks for third-party mods on Forge 1.19.2.** Creative Compat changes how other mods work together: Quark, Supplementaries, Handcrafted, Create, Immersive Portals, Polymorph, Sophisticated Backpacks, Corpse, JEI, WTHIT, FTB Quests and about 50 more. A mod's tweaks only switch on when that mod is installed, and only at a version it was tested with. It also holds a few changes that need no other mod: walking through crops, snow and vines, block walking speeds, falling snow layers, an iron golem farm cooldown and boat-only block friction. Each of those has an on/off switch.

***

## Requirements

|                        |Version                               |
| ---------------------- |------------------------------------- |
| Minecraft              |1.19.2                                |
| Forge                  |43 or newer                           |
| Creative Common        |1.3.0 or newer                        |
| KubeJS                 |1902.6.2-build.73 or newer (1902.6.x), required by Creative Common |
| Rhino and Architectury |required by KubeJS                    |

Everything else is optional; see "Optional mods: what changes with each" below.

Both client and server need it. Client-only parts (tooltips, JEI and WTHIT changes, rendering) do nothing on a dedicated server, and server-only parts do nothing on the client.

***

## What it does on its own

These features need no other third-party mod. Each one that changes vanilla behavior has a switch in the `[mixins]` section of `config/creativecompat-common.toml`, or its own section, and all are on by default. A switch in `[mixins]` is read at game start, so restart after changing one.

### Walking and movement

<div class="spoiler"><ul><li><strong>Block walking speed.</strong> Every block belongs to a surface tier and changes how fast you walk on it: asphalt 1.5 times, concrete 1.4, grating 1.3, metal, ice, cut stone and bricks 1.25, trodden paths 1.15, wood 1.1, raw rock 1.05, plain blocks 1.0, then rough ground 0.92, soft blocks 0.88, sand and dust 0.85, farmland 0.8, leaves 0.78, snow 0.75, sticky pistons 0.65, mud and clay 0.6, and slime and honey blocks 0.45. Tier members are block tags, so any mod's blocks can be sorted. Speeds are in <code>[surface.speed]</code>; the server's values are sent to players. The field of view no longer changes with these speeds.</li><li><strong>Framed and copycat blocks</strong> take the walking speed of the block they copy (the one inserted most recently in a mixed block). Switch: <code>[surface.camouflage] copyCamo</code>.</li><li><strong>Walking through plants.</strong> Crops, wild crops, vines, tall plants, sugar cane, ropes, cobwebs, berry bushes, snow layers, leaf piles and bamboo leaves slow you down and soften falls as you move through them, per tier in <code>[surface.passthrough.&lt;tier&gt;]</code>. Cobwebs can hold arrows, fireworks and items, and a surrounded web can be climbed. Switch: <code>[surface.passthrough] enabled</code>.</li><li><strong>Snow layers</strong> can be walked through, fall like sand, and are replaced by a placed block up to a set depth (<code>replaceMaxLayers</code>, default 3). Jumps are weaker inside deep snow and leaf layers, mobs sunk in snow climb out when they walk into a block, and mobs do not path through deep leaf piles.</li><li><strong>Sinking.</strong> Entities standing on full soft, dust, spongy or very sticky blocks are drawn slightly lower, and so is the camera, like soul sand. Only the picture changes; hitboxes and movement do not. Setting: <code>[surface.sinking] depthPixels</code> (client).</li><li><strong>Boat friction.</strong> A block list sets how slippery blocks are for boats only; players and other entities keep the block's own slipperiness. Section: <code>[boat_friction]</code>.</li></ul></div>

### Vanilla behavior

<div class="spoiler"><ul><li><strong>Iron golem farm cooldown.</strong> When 5 iron golems die in one village within 10 minutes, the village summons no golems for 1 in-game day. Golems built by players neither count nor are affected. Section: <code>[golems]</code>.</li><li><strong>Moss color.</strong> Moss blocks and moss carpet take the biome's grass color (client). Section: <code>[moss]</code>.</li><li><strong>Sheared vines.</strong> Shears on the tip of a weeping, twisting or cave vine or on kelp stop it growing, as in 1.20. Section: <code>[vine_shearing]</code>.</li><li><strong>Berries</strong> (sweet and glow) are listed in the creative tabs and JEI again.</li><li><strong>TNT shrapnel.</strong> A primed or burning TNT also flings out a burst of fast projectiles (default 24, 3 damage). Section: <code>[shrapnel]</code>.</li><li><strong>Falling blocks splash</strong> when they enter water (client).</li><li><strong>Screen and interface details</strong> (client): a tooltip taller than the screen keeps its top visible (<code>[tooltips]</code>), the world behind screens can be dimmed (<code>[screens]</code>), tutorial and advancement toasts can be hidden (<code>[toasts]</code>), sign text is not drawn far from the camera, and resource pack title plaques stretch to long container titles (<code>[gui_title]</code>).</li><li><strong>Weathered cardboard box</strong>: a decorative block.</li></ul></div>

## Optional pack settings

<div class="spoiler"><p>Out of the box, every tweak is on. Any pack can change these in <code>config/creativecompat-common.toml</code>:</p><table><thead><tr><th>Setting</th><th>What it does</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><code>[mixins]</code></td><td>One on/off key per change that alters vanilla behavior (32 keys); restart to apply</td></tr><tr><td><code>[surface.speed]</code></td><td>Walk speed per surface tier (0.05 to 4.0)</td></tr><tr><td><code>[surface.passthrough]</code></td><td>Master switch, arrow lifetime (12000 ticks) and one table per plant tier: walk and fall factors, neighbor rule, items and projectiles</td></tr><tr><td><code>[golems]</code></td><td>Deaths (5), window (12000 ticks) and cooldown (24000 ticks) of the golem farm limit</td></tr><tr><td><code>[boat_friction]</code></td><td>Block list for boat friction</td></tr><tr><td><code>[shrapnel]</code></td><td>Count, speed, damage, item and range of TNT shrapnel</td></tr><tr><td><code>[jei] pagesOff</code></td><td>Recipe pages that other Creative mods gather (seasoning, cans, potion buckets, tipped arrows) to leave off; empty by default</td></tr><tr><td><code>[jei] craftableFirst</code>, <code>packOrder</code>, <code>fluidAmountScale</code></td><td>JEI recipe order, ingredient order from the pack's definition (off by default) and how full fluids look in tanks</td></tr><tr><td><code>[corpses]</code></td><td>Decay times and sinking of Corpse mod corpses, and the sectioned corpse screen</td></tr><tr><td><code>[trash_slot]</code>, <code>[monster_box]</code>, <code>[cats]</code>, <code>[giant_squid]</code>, <code>[vein_undo]</code>, <code>[paintings]</code></td><td>Settings for the matching optional-mod changes below</td></tr><tr><td><code>[wthit]</code></td><td>One switch for each added WTHIT line</td></tr><tr><td><code>[immersive_portals]</code>, <code>[portalgun]</code></td><td>Portal render distances, fog and Portal Gun portal settings</td></tr><tr><td><code>[debug]</code></td><td>Developer switches, all off</td></tr></tbody></table><p>With Create installed, the file can also be edited in game through Create's config screen.</p></div>

## Optional mods: what changes with each

<div class="spoiler"><p>None of these are required. Each change only switches on when that mod is installed. Changes that alter another mod's code are tied to the versions given (the tested version up to the next minor version); with any other version they are turned off and one warning is logged, so an update to that mod never crashes the game.</p>

<p><b>Storage, crafting and furniture</b></p><ul><li><strong>Polymorph (0.46.x)</strong><ul><li>Recipe choice works in Sophisticated Backpacks' crafting upgrades (with Sophisticated Core 0.6.4), Immersive Engineering's crafting table and Pretty Pipes' crafting terminal.</li></ul></li><li><strong>TrashSlot (12.1.x)</strong><ul><li>The trash slot can be turned on or off per screen and moved; each Sophisticated Backpacks tier remembers its own position. Section: <code>[trash_slot]</code>.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Handcrafted (2.0.x)</strong><ul><li>Nightstand storage and held items save correctly.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Supplementaries (2.4.20)</strong><ul><li>Jars show their contents in WTHIT and tooltips, fill and mix, and pipes can fill them; dyed blocks, flower box plants and sack pick-up are fixed or extended; ash and fodder layers behave like snow layers.</li><li>With Breezy (1.0.8): a wind vane crash is fixed.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Quark (3.4)</strong><ul><li>Sorting no longer loses items; monster boxes can act as vaults (a reward and a cooldown of 1 hour, one wave at a time). Section: <code>[monster_box]</code>.</li><li>Pickarang and torch arrow handling adjusted for pass-through blocks.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Another Furniture (2.1.x)</strong><ul><li>Planter models draw their plants (client).</li></ul></li><li><strong>Framed Blocks (6.8.x), Copycats+ (2.2.x)</strong><ul><li>Double-material blocks order their camo by most recent, for walking speed; flower pots in frames can be unframed.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Beautify (1.4.x), Botany Pots (9.0.x), Twigs (3.0.x)</strong><ul><li>Botanist workbenches placed by structures start with a flower, Botany Pots grow in the listed dimensions, and Twigs' silt pots take their soil. Bamboo leaves can be walked through.</li></ul></li></ul>

<p><b>Create and Immersive Engineering</b></p><ul><li><strong>Create (0.5.1)</strong><ul><li>Create's block breaker skips Quark monster boxes (with Quark); the Create config screen can open sections.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Create: Dreams &amp; Desires (1.2c)</strong><ul><li>Jetpack tooltip fixed.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Immersive Engineering (9.2.4)</strong><ul><li>Sawdust layers fall and can be walked through.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Immersive Weathering (1.2.x)</strong><ul><li>Leaf piles, sand layers and azalea flowers behave like other layers (azalea flowers with Twigs).</li></ul></li></ul>

<p><b>Dropped items</b></p><ul><li><strong>ItemPhysic (1.6.7)</strong><ul><li>Items that sink, float, burn in fire or burn in lava are chosen by item tag (see above). Burning items can transform or explode (with shrapnel), and Allurement curses are handled.</li></ul></li></ul>

<p><b>Animals, villagers and companions</b></p><ul><li><strong>Domestication Innovation (1.6.x)</strong><ul><li>The Feather on a Stick can hook mobs. Hostile mobs on by default; neutral and passive mobs and players off. Section: <code>[cats]</code>.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Domestication Innovation with Cozy's Improved Cats (2.1.x)</strong><ul><li>Cats follow and attack a held feather, and get a buff from it (10 seconds, level 3 by default).</li></ul></li><li><strong>Alex's Mobs (1.21.x)</strong><ul><li>Giant squids flee from whales. Section: <code>[giant_squid]</code>.</li></ul></li><li><strong>MCA Reborn (7.5.x)</strong><ul><li>Villagers path over leaf piles; eye colors on combs fixed.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Simple Hats (0.3.x)</strong><ul><li>Hats on the face slot.</li></ul></li></ul>

<p><b>Corpses</b></p><ul><li><strong>Corpse (1.0.17)</strong><ul><li>Corpses decay by game time, also while unloaded: a corpse holding items becomes a skeleton after 7 days (1 day in the Nether, then a wither skeleton), an emptied one after 30 seconds and is gone after 5 minutes; in the End it never decays. Skeletons sink in water and lava. Section: <code>[corpses]</code>.</li><li>The corpse screen shows cosmetic armor and curios in their own slots (with Curios 5.1.x), and both models wear them. Transfer Items returns curios to their slots.</li></ul></li></ul>

<p><b>Mining and maps</b></p><ul><li><strong>Ore Excavation (1.11.x)</strong><ul><li><code>/undo_excavation</code> has a cooldown (5 seconds) and refuses when the vein mine broke ores, storage blocks, spawners or blocks with inventories. Section: <code>[vein_undo]</code>.</li></ul></li><li><strong>BlockRunner (4.2.x)</strong><ul><li>Speeds come from <code>[surface.speed]</code>, and the field of view stays steady.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Xaero's World Map (1.40.x)</strong><ul><li>Map block colors fixed.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Distant Horizons (3.0.x)</strong><ul><li>Cloud height and generation rules changed.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Botania (440)</strong><ul><li>Chakrams pass through plants.</li></ul></li></ul>

<p><b>Portals</b></p><ul><li><strong>Immersive Portals (2.3.x)</strong><ul><li>Mirror and portal render limits and fog settings. Section: <code>[immersive_portals]</code>.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Portal Gun (5.0.x)</strong><ul><li>Opaque portals optional, a despawn distance and heartbeat, a dye recipe and its JEI page. Section: <code>[portalgun]</code>.</li></ul></li><li><strong>GeckoLib (3.1.x) with ImmediatelyFast (1.4.x)</strong><ul><li>Animated items in the hotbar no longer break the HUD.</li></ul></li></ul>

<p><b>Paintings and banners</b></p><ul><li><strong>Immersive Paintings (0.6.x)</strong><ul><li>Every painting variant of the installed mods is offered, built at load from their own art; old painting ids are kept. Section: <code>[paintings]</code>.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Bedspreads (6.0.x), Customizable Elytra (1.7.x)</strong><ul><li>Their banner patterns are built at load from the installed jars.</li></ul></li></ul>

<p><b>JEI, WTHIT and tooltips (client)</b></p><ul><li><strong>JEI</strong><ul><li>Recipes whose + button works are listed first, fluids in tanks scale by a curve so small amounts stay visible, recipe and ingredient tooltips are fixed, and an optional pack ingredient order. Section: <code>[jei]</code>.</li></ul></li><li><strong>Just Enough Effect Descriptions (2.1.x)</strong><ul><li>Effect descriptions and icons.</li></ul></li><li><strong>WTHIT</strong><ul><li>Lines for jar and pot contents, beehives, block state, walking speed, silk touch and sheared drops, operator blocks, campfires and more; each has a switch in <code>[wthit]</code>.</li></ul></li><li><strong>FTB Library (1902.4.x), FTB Quests (1902.5.x), Patchouli (77), Applied Energistics 2 (12.9.x), Wares (1.2.x), Pretty Pipes (1.13.x), Lightman's Currency (2.2.x), Legendary Tooltips (1.4.x) with Iceberg</strong><ul><li>Item tooltips keep their top on screen and use the pack's tooltip lines; Lightman's Currency screens and Legendary Tooltips title breaks are fixed.</li></ul></li></ul></div>
