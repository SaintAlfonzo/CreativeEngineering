# Spice of Life: Raspberry Pie Addition 1.0.0 (CurseForge file changelog)

First release.

- Diet bonuses on top of Spice of Life: Apple Pie Edition: a ladder of rewards by food diversity (extra hearts, armor toughness, knockback resistance, permanent effects, effect slots and one-time effect unlocks).
- Effect slots hold an effect of your choice from the ladder's pool. Changing a slot to a different effect ages out the 5 oldest foods of the diet window.
- The Food Book is renamed Diet Log and gains overview, Diet Ladder and Milestones pages.
- Drinks stay out of the food diet and are recorded in their own book, the Liquid Diet Log.
- The ladder is one editable file per world. Mods and packs can set the diversity of their foods with data files.
- Operator commands under `/solrp`.
- Spice of Life: Apple Pie Edition 2.2.0 and Spice of Life: Carrot Edition 1.14.0 are bundled unmodified inside the jar.

The project page lists every feature and its settings.

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.19.2, Forge 43 or newer. Apple Pie is bundled; Creative Common and KubeJS are not needed. Both client and server need it, at the same version.
