Hearth and Harvest 1.4.1 (NeoForge 1.21.1, by AlabasterLeking), the source of the art and data in backportedHearth
(Creative Delight).
Source: the Hearth and Harvest 1.4.1 release jar (hearthandharvest-1.21.1-1.4.1.jar, sha1
2cfe15e8e6cfe492e2e3d2ff19af775b8df4e321). Its META-INF/neoforge.mods.toml declares license="MIT License" and
authors="AlabasterLeking". The licence text below is the standard MIT License text.

Taken from that jar, unchanged unless noted: the raspberry_bush blockstate, its four block models and the crop_cross
parent model, the four bush textures, the
raspberry and raspberry_pips item models and textures, the raspberry_bush block loot table (1.21-only
"random_sequence" removed), the English names of raspberry, raspberry_pips and raspberry_bush, the watering_can
texture (used by every watering can tier for now) and the watering can tooltip and fertilizer strings. The
watering can Java is adapted from 1.4.1's WateringCanItem (MIT); the item models are plain generated models.
The Java code is our own, written for Forge 1.19.2 from the 1.4.1 behaviour, and is GNU GPL3 like the rest of the
pack's code.
