Ender's Delight 1.1.4 (Forge 1.20.1, by Axedgaming; credits: Axedgaming, Furti Two), the source of the art and data in
endersAdditions (Creative Delight).
Source: the Ender's Delight 1.1.4 release jar (endersdelight-1.1.4.jar, sha1 d1541d70c7dbae71c2a7e1693f512f2166908802).
The jar ships no LICENSE file; its META-INF/mods.toml declares license="MIT" and authors="Axedgaming". The licence text
below is the standard MIT License text.

Taken from that jar, unchanged unless noted: the blockstates, block and item models, block and item textures, block loot
tables (1.20-only "random_sequence" removed) and the English names of amberveil_mushroom, ethereal_saffron_bush,
chorus_flame_block, voidpepper_block and their items, crushed_voidpepper, amberveil_stew, amberveiled_curry,
chicken_curry, steak_fries and veil_of_flames_risotto. The voidpepper_block blockstate is changed so the plain and the
fruiting vine models no longer overlap. Also from that jar: the End House (structures/end_house.nbt and its
structure, template pool and structure set files; the structure type changed to minecraft:jigsaw) and the chorus_crate
and endstone_stove block loot tables (the crate's gained the block type and an explosion condition). Nothing in this jar comes from the installed Ender's Delight 1.2.2 (All Rights
Reserved). The Java code is our own, written from the 1.1.4 behaviour, and is GNU GPL3 like the rest of the pack's code.
