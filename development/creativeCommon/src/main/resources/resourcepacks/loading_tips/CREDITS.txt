Loading Tips (resource pack)

The tip text in assets/loading_tips/loading_tips/tips.json is taken from Loading Tips 1.2.1+26.1
by Naw7k (https://modrinth.com/mod/loading-tips, https://github.com/Naw7k/Loading-Tips), licensed
under the MIT License. For Creative Engineering it was filtered to tips that hold in Minecraft 1.19.2:
tips about later versions, duplicates and incorrect tips were removed and one tip was reworded.
The full list of removals is in the pack's feature writeup (feature_writeup/loading_tips.md).

The tips are shown by Creative Common's loading screen tips feature, which follows the format of
Loading Screen Tips by UltrusBot (https://github.com/UltrusBot/LoadingScreenTips, MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2022 UltrusBot).
