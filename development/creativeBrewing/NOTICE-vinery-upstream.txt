textures, models, sounds, lang, recipes, tags, loot tables or other resources. Those are read at runtime from the
unmodified upstream jars, which must be present in <game dir>/vinery_upstream/:
  letsdo-vinery-fabric-1.5.5.jar  (https://modrinth.com/mod/lets-do-vinery, version 1.5.5 for Fabric 1.21.1)
  letsdo-vinery-forge-1.3.10.jar  (https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lets-do-vinery, 1.3.10 for Forge 1.19.2)
